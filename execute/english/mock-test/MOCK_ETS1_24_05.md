### 🆕 GRAMMAR CHEAT SHEET - TỪ PART 5 ETS:

```
ĐẠI TỪ:
  of + _____ + N → possessive adj (her/his/my/their)
  both...but _____ → neither (2 người, không ai)
  both...and _____ → each/both

LIÊN TỪ vs GIỚI TỪ:
  _____ + CLAUSE (S+V) → so that / even though / although
  _____ + NOUN          → despite / because of / due to

  ⚠️ BẪY: even though ≠ despite
     even though + the cost WAS high ✅
     despite + the high cost ✅
     despite + the cost was high ❌

TỪ LOẠI:
  $95 _____ fee → Adj + N → diagnostic
  parking _____ have been met → N (chủ ngữ) → restrictions

COLLOCATION (phải thuộc):
  role IN doing sth
  compensate FOR sth
  absorb water/heat/information
  rather than = thay vì
```
