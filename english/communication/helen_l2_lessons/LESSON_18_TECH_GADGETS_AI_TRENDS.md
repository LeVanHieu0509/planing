|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How has artificial intelligence transformed your daily work? | AI tools have dramatically accelerated my coding speed, automated test generation, and made debugging complex logic 3 times faster. | Present Perfect: `have transformed` |
| 2 | Do you think AI will replace software engineers in the future? | I don't believe AI will replace engineers entirely; rather, engineers who leverage AI will replace those who don't. | Comparative contrast |

| 4 | How do you ensure you don't over-rely on AI answers? | I treat AI as a junior assistant: I always review the generated output, verify edge cases, and test thoroughly before deployment. | Simile / Metaphor: `treat as` |

## 6. 💡 PRO SPEAKING STRATEGY - THE 3-STEP RESPONSE

- **Câu hỏi**: *"How do you personally balance technology usage with real-world relationships?"*
- **Bước 1 (Direct Boundary)**: *"I establish clear physical and digital boundaries between screen time and personal presence."*
- **Bước 2 (Concrete Routine)**: *"For instance, during dinner with family or friends, I implement a 'no-phones-on-the-table' rule so everyone engages in genuine, eye-to-eye conversations."*
- **Bước 3 (Philosophical Summary)**: *"Technology should be a tool that serves human connection, not a barrier that replaces it."*
