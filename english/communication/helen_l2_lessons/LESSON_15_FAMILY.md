## 📌 MỤC TIÊU BUỔI HỌC

- **Làm chủ cấu trúc Cây Gia Phả 4 thế hệ (The 4-Generation Family Tree)**: Phân biệt rõ nhánh nội (`Paternal`) và ngoại (`Maternal`), các thế hệ từ Tổ tiên / Cụ (`Ancestors`, `Great-grandparents`) ➔ Ông bà (`Grandparents`) ➔ Cha mẹ (`Parents`, `Dad/Papa`, `Mom/Mama`) & Cô dì chú bác (`Uncle`, `Aunt`) ➔ Anh chị em ruột (`Siblings`), Anh chị em họ (`Cousins`), Họ hàng đằng vợ/chồng (`-in-law`) ➔ Con cái (`Son`, `Daughter`, `Adopted child`) & Cháu ruột (`Nephew`, `Niece`).
- **Ghi nhớ và phản xạ 8 định nghĩa cốt lõi từ bảng lớp học**: `Nephew` (Brother or sister's son), `Niece` (Brother or sister's daughter), `Family reunion` (Big gathering with your family members), `Siblings` (Brother or sister), `Adopt` (Take care of a child that is not yours), `Ancestors` (People in your family that have died), `Relatives` (People in your family that are alive), `Half-brother / sister` (Share only one parent).
- **Thành thạo cặp cấu trúc so sánh thế hệ đắt giá**: Phân biệt chuẩn xác giữa **`Look like`** (ngoại hình, đường nét khuôn mặt, nụ cười) và **`Act like` / `Take after`** (tính cách, khí chất, thói quen, cách xử lý vấn đề trong cuộc sống).
- **Thực hành phản xạ lưu loát toàn bộ 12 câu hỏi Worksheet** theo công thức 3 bước (*Direct Answer ➔ Detailed Reason ➔ Personal Example*), kết hợp nhuần nhuyễn cấu trúc giả định `Would you ever...?` từ Buổi 14 (*Would you ever adopt a child?*).
- **Nâng cấp phát âm & Giảm âm chuẩn bản xứ**: Phân biệt cặp từ dễ nhầm lẫn trên bảng `Niece` /niːs/ (âm xì /s/ nhẹ) vs `Knees` /niːz/ (đầu gối, âm rung /z/), kỹ thuật giảm âm trong `Family` /ˈfæm.li/ (2 âm tiết).

## 3. 🔍 KEY GRAMMAR: "LOOK LIKE" VS "ACT LIKE" & "WOULD YOU EVER"

### 🎯 A. Phân biệt: `Look like` (Ngoại hình) vs `Act like` / `Take after` (Tính cách):

```
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. LOOK LIKE  ➔ Dùng để miêu tả NGOẠI HÌNH, KHUÔN MẶT, VÓC DÁNG BÊN NGOÀI
   • Cấu trúc : S + look(s) like + [someone]
   • Hoặc     : S + have/has [facial feature] like + [someone]
   • Ví dụ 1  : "I look like my father. We have the exact same smile and nose."
                (Tôi có ngoại hình giống bố. Chúng tôi có nụ cười và chiếc mũi y hệt nhau.)
   • Ví dụ 2  : "My daughter looks like her grandmother when she laughs."

2. ACT LIKE   ➔ Dùng để miêu tả HÀNH VI, TÍNH CÁCH, THÓI QUEN, CÁCH ỨNG XỬ
   • Cấu trúc : S + act(s) like + [someone]
   • Hoặc     : S + take(s) after + [someone] in terms of [personality trait]
   • Ví dụ 1  : "I act like my mother because I am very patient and organized."
                (Tôi hành xử giống mẹ vì tôi rất kiên nhẫn và ngăn nắp.)
   • Ví dụ 2  : "My younger brother acts like my uncle; both love cracking funny jokes!"
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
```

### 🔮 B. Kết nối cấu trúc giả định `Would you ever adopt a child?` (Buổi 14 ➔ Buổi 15):

```
       ▲  [ 100% ]  "Yes, absolutely! I would love to give a warm home to a child."

       │  [ 10%  ]  "Probably not, as raising an adopted child requires immense dedication."

```

## 4. 💬 12 WORKSHEET QUESTIONS & 3-STEP MODEL ANSWERS

Dưới đây là 12 câu hỏi đàm thoại chính thức trên phiếu học tập kèm câu trả lời mẫu 3 bước chuyên nghiệp (*Direct Answer ➔ Detailed Reason ➔ Personal Example*):

---

### 1. How many people are there in your family?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"There are **four people** in my immediate family: my parents, my younger sister, and myself. We live in a cozy apartment in Ho Chi Minh City. Even though everyone has a busy working schedule during weekdays, we always make sure to sit down together for dinner every evening."_
- **Phân tích:** Trả lời trực tiếp số lượng thành viên (`four people in my immediate family`) ➔ Liệt kê chi tiết từng thành viên (`parents, younger sister, myself`) ➔ Hoàn cảnh gia đình ấm áp ăn tối mỗi ngày.
- **Từ vựng đắt giá:** `immediate family` (gia đình trực hệ), `cozy apartment`, `busy working schedule`.

---

### 2. Describe your ideal husband or wife?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"My ideal spouse would be someone who is **emotionally mature, kind-hearted, and shares similar core values**. Above all, mutual respect and open communication are essential. I would love a partner who has her own career ambition, but still values family warmth and enjoys cooking relaxing weekend meals together."_
- **Phân tích:** Trả lời trực tiếp 3 phẩm chất quan trọng (`emotionally mature, kind-hearted, shared core values`) ➔ Nền tảng mối quan hệ (`mutual respect, open communication`) ➔ Ví dụ đời sống cân bằng giữa công việc và tổ ấm.
- **Từ vựng đắt giá:** `ideal spouse`, `core values`, `mutual respect`, `career ambition`.

---

### 3. How many siblings do you have? Who is the oldest and who is the youngest?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"I have **one sibling**, a younger sister who is three years younger than me. That makes me the **oldest child** in the family, while my sister is the **youngest baby** of the house. Growing up, we used to bicker over tiny things like TV channels, but now that we are adults, she is one of my closest confidantes and best friends."_
- **Phân tích:** Trả lời trực tiếp số lượng (`one sibling, younger sister`) ➔ Phân định thứ bậc (`oldest child vs youngest baby`) ➔ Tình cảm anh em từ lúc cãi nhau đến khi gắn kết tri kỷ (`closest confidante`).
- **Từ vựng đắt giá:** `oldest child`, `baby of the house`, `bicker over`, `closest confidante`.

---

### 4. Do you have any children? Do you want to have children in the future?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"I **don't have any children yet** as I am currently focusing on advancing my professional career and personal financial readiness. However, I **definitely want to have two children in the future** — ideally a boy and a girl. Being a parent is a tremendous responsibility, but guiding a child into a kind, capable human being is the most meaningful milestone in life."_
- **Phân tích:** Nêu hiện trạng thực tế (`don't have children yet`) ➔ Dự định tương lai cụ thể (`want two children: a boy and a girl`) ➔ Quan điểm về thiên chức làm cha mẹ (`tremendous responsibility, meaningful milestone`).
- **Từ vựng đắt giá:** `financial readiness`, `tremendous responsibility`, `meaningful milestone`.

---

### 5. Would you ever adopt a child?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"**Yes, I might, but it depends on the circumstances!** If my future partner and I are financially comfortable and emotionally ready to provide unconditional love, I would gladly consider adopting a child who has lost their parents. Adoption is an extraordinarily noble act that gives an underprivileged kid a bright, loving future."_
- **Phân tích:** Ứng dụng thang phản xạ Buổi 14 (`Yes, I might, but it depends...`) ➔ Điều kiện cần có (`financially comfortable, emotionally ready`) ➔ Ý nghĩa nhân văn cao đẹp (`extraordinarily noble act, underprivileged kid`).
- **Từ vựng đắt giá:** `unconditional love`, `financially comfortable`, `noble act`, `underprivileged child`.

---

### 6. How often do you have a family reunion? What do you do at a family reunion?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"We usually host a grand family reunion **twice a year**, most notably during the **Lunar New Year (Tết Holiday)** and our **ancestor memorial day in the summer**. During the reunion, all the aunts, uncles, and cousins gather at my grandparents' house. We prepare a massive traditional feast, exchange warm life updates, give lucky money to the kids, and sing joyful karaoke together until late afternoon."_
- **Phân tích:** Xác định tần suất rõ ràng (`twice a year, during Tet and ancestor memorial day`) ➔ Liệt kê các hoạt động náo nhiệt (`traditional feast, lucky money, karaoke`) ➔ Không khí sum vầy đầm ấm.
- **Từ vựng đắt giá:** `grand family reunion`, `ancestor memorial day`, `massive traditional feast`, `exchange life updates`.

---

### 7. Who is the oldest person in your family? Tell me about him or her.
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"The oldest living person in our family is my **maternal grandmother**, who is celebrating her **88th birthday** this year. Despite her advanced age, she remains remarkably sharp, gentle, and warm-spirited. She has lived through historical eras and always shares captivating stories about traditional Vietnamese village life and the importance of resilience."_
- **Phân tích:** Giới thiệu nhân vật và độ tuổi (`maternal grandmother, 88th birthday`) ➔ Sức khỏe và tinh thần minh mẫn (`remarkably sharp, gentle, warm-spirited`) ➔ Bài học truyền cảm hứng cho con cháu (`captivating stories, resilience`).
- **Từ vựng đắt giá:** `maternal grandmother`, `advanced age`, `remarkably sharp`, `resilience`.

---

### 8. Talk about some fun activities you can do with your family.
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"There are several delightful activities we love doing together. On regular weekends, we love having an **outdoor barbecue in the backyard** or playing lively **board games like Uno and Monopoly**. During long holidays, our favorite tradition is taking **spontaneous road trips to the beach or mountain towns**, where we can hike, take scenic photos, and escape busy city work."_
- **Phân tích:** Đa dạng hóa hoạt động thường nhật và kỳ nghỉ ➔ Hoạt động cuối tuần (`outdoor barbecue, board games`) ➔ Kế hoạch dã ngoại xa (`spontaneous road trips, escape busy work`).
- **Từ vựng đắt giá:** `outdoor barbecue`, `lively board games`, `spontaneous road trips`, `scenic photos`.

---

### 9. Would you prefer to live with your parents or live alone? Why?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"At this stage in my life, **I prefer living independently**, although I deeply love my parents. Living on my own allows me to develop full self-reliance, manage my personal budget, and maintain focused work hours for my engineering projects. However, I always make it a strict priority to visit my parents every weekend so that we never lose our tight family connection."_
- **Phân tích:** Khẳng định lập trường độc lập (`prefer living independently`) ➔ 3 lý do thực tế (`self-reliance, personal budget, focused work hours`) ➔ Giải pháp dung hòa thăm bố mẹ hàng tuần (`visit every weekend, tight family connection`).
- **Từ vựng đắt giá:** `live independently`, `self-reliance`, `personal budget`, `tight family connection`.

---

### 10. Do you know anything about your ancestors?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"Yes, our family maintains a very detailed **genealogy book (gia phả)**. According to our ancestral records, our family originated from a peaceful farming village in Central Vietnam before relocating south decades ago. My ancestors were hardworking artisans and dedicated teachers who instilled a strict respect for continuous education and integrity across all subsequent generations."_
- **Phân tích:** Nguồn gốc gia phả (`genealogy book / ancestral records`) ➔ Nguồn cội địa lý (`farming village in Central Vietnam`) ➔ Giá trị đạo đức và truyền thống hiếu học trao truyền qua các thế hệ (`hardworking artisans, integrity, continuous education`).
- **Từ vựng đắt giá:** `genealogy book`, `ancestral records`, `hardworking artisans`, `integrity`.

---

### 11. Which relative(s) do you look like?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"Everyone in my extended family agrees that **I look remarkably like my father**. We have the exact same sharp jawline, high forehead, and deep-set eyes. Whenever relatives see old vintage photos of my father in his twenties, they often mistake him for me because the facial resemblance is uncanny!"_
- **Phân tích:** Khẳng định giống ai về ngoại hình (`look remarkably like my father`) ➔ Mô tả chi tiết các đường nét giải phẫu (`sharp jawline, high forehead, deep-set eyes`) ➔ Bằng chứng hài hước khi xem ảnh cũ (`uncanny resemblance`).
- **Từ vựng đắt giá:** `look remarkably like`, `sharp jawline`, `deep-set eyes`, `uncanny resemblance`.

---

### 12. Which relative(s) do you act like?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"In terms of personality and behavior, **I definitely act like my mother**. Both of us are very calm under high pressure, empathetic listeners, and meticulous planners who always organize schedules well in advance. When unexpected problems occur, like my mother, I take a deep breath and search for logical solutions rather than panicking."_
- **Phân tích:** Xác định giống ai về khí chất (`definitely act like my mother`) ➔ 3 đặc điểm tính cách nổi bật (`calm under high pressure, empathetic listener, meticulous planner`) ➔ Phản xạ thực tế khi gặp sự cố (`search for logical solutions rather than panicking`).
- **Từ vựng đắt giá:** `act like`, `calm under high pressure`, `empathetic listener`, `meticulous planner`.

## 6. 🔤 PRONUNCIATION CLINIC & NATIVE INTONATION (KHOA PHÁT ÂM CHUẨN)

### ⚠️ A. Cặp từ dễ gây nhầm lẫn: "Niece" vs "Knees" (Ghi chú trên bảng của Giáo viên):

