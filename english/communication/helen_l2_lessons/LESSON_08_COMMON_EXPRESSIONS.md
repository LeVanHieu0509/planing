## 1. 💡 9 COMMON EXPRESSIONS - BẢNG THÀNH NGỮ PHỔ BIẾN

| #   | Expression               | Detailed Meaning                               | Vietnamese Meaning                | Example Sentence                                        |
| --- | ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Sick and tired**       | Extremely annoyed / frustrated with something  | Chán ngấy / Mệt mỏi với điều gì   | _"I am **sick and tired** of traffic jams in HCMC."_    |
| 2   | **7 o'clock sharp**      | Exactly at 7:00 (not 7:01 or 6:59)             | Đúng 7 giờ (không trễ)            | _"The meeting starts at **7 o'clock sharp**."_          |
| 3   | **Give me a hand**       | Help me with something physical or work        | Giúp tôi một tay                  | _"Could you **give me a hand** with this heavy box?"_   |
| 4   | **I'm broke**            | Having no money at all                         | Cháy túi / Hết tiền               | _"I can't go to the luxury restaurant, **I'm broke**!"_ |
| 5   | **Get the ball rolling** | Start an activity or project                   | Bắt đầu tiến hành / Khởi động     | _"Let's **get the ball rolling** on the new feature."_  |
| 6   | **Take it easy**         | Relax, don't stress / work too hard            | Thư giãn / Bình tĩnh / Từ từ làm  | _"Don't stress too much! Just **take it easy**."_       |
| 7   | **Under the weather**    | Feeling slightly sick or unwell                | Thấy không khỏe / Ốm nhẹ          | _"I'm feeling a bit **under the weather** today."_      |
| 8   | **On the same page**     | Agreeing / Having the same understanding       | Cùng chung quan điểm / Hiểu nhau  | _"We need to make sure we are **on the same page**."_   |
| 9   | **Speak of the devil**   | The person you were just talking about arrives | Vừa nhắc Tào Tháo là Tào Tháo tới | _"**Speak of the devil**! Here comes Nam."_             |

## 2. ❓ QUESTIONS & MODEL ANSWERS - CÂU HỎI & MẪU TRẢ LỜI

### 📊 Bảng câu hỏi & Mẫu trả lời tham khảo:

| #   | Question                                                                                 | Model Answer                                                                                                 | Idiom Used             |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------- |
| 1   | **If you were broke, what would you do for fun? What would you eat?**                    | If I **were broke**, I would stay at home, read tech blogs, or practice coding. I would eat instant noodles! | `I'm broke`            |
| 2   | **Do you think it is possible to be broke and happy at the same time?**                  | Yes, I think happiness comes from healthy relationships and mindset, not just money.                         | `broke`                |
| 3   | **What do you usually do when you're taking it easy?**                                   | When I am **taking it easy**, I drink acoustic coffee or listen to podcast at night.                         | `take it easy`         |
| 4   | **What are you sick and tired of?**                                                      | I am **sick and tired** of rainy season traffic jams during rush hour in Saigon.                             | `sick and tired`       |
| 5   | **Do you ever feel sick and tired of living in Ho Chi Minh City?**                       | Sometimes because of noise, but overall I love HCMC because of career opportunities in tech.                 | `sick and tired`       |
| 6   | **Do you think people should stay home or go to work when they feel under the weather?** | I think they should stay home or work remotely to rest and avoid spreading germs.                            | `under the weather`    |
| 7   | **Have you ever lied about 'being under the weather' to your boss?**                     | No, I haven't. I always try to be transparent and honest with my manager.                                    | `under the weather`    |
| 8   | **How would you get the ball rolling when starting a conversation with someone new?**    | I would **get the ball rolling** by giving a polite compliment or asking about their interests.              | `get the ball rolling` |
| 9   | **Are you always on the same page with your co-workers?**                                | Most of the time yes, but if we have different ideas, we discuss until we are **on the same page**.          | `on the same page`     |

## 3. 📚 EXPANDED VOCABULARY - TỪ VỰNG MỞ RỘNG BỔ SUNG IDIOMS & THÀNH NGỮ THÔNG DỤNG

| Vocabulary                    | IPA                   | Word Type | Meaning                        | Example Sentence                                                   |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------- | --------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Hit the nail on the head**  | /hɪt ðə neɪl/         | idiom     | Nói chính xác trọng tâm        | _"You **hit the nail on the head** with that explanation!"_        |
| **Burn the midnight oil**     | /bɜːn ðə ˈmɪd.naɪt/   | idiom     | Thức khuya làm việc / cày code | _"I had to **burn the midnight oil** to fix the production bug."_  |
| **See eye to eye**            | /siː aɪ tuː aɪ/       | idiom     | Đồng quan điểm với ai          | _"We don't always **see eye to eye**, but we respect each other."_ |
| **Out of the blue**           | /aʊt əv ðə bluː/      | idiom     | Bất ngờ / Đột ngột             | _"He called me **out of the blue** after 3 years."_                |
| **Once in a blue moon**       | /wʌns ɪn ə bluː/      | idiom     | Rất hiếm khi                   | _"I only eat fast food **once in a blue moon**."_                  |
| **Call it a day**             | /kɔːl ɪt ə deɪ/       | idiom     | Nghỉ tay / Dừng công việc      | _"It's 8 PM, let's **call it a day** and go home."_                |
| **Back to the drawing board** | /bæk tuː ðə ˈdrɔː.ɪŋ/ | idiom     | Làm lại từ đầu                 | _"The build failed, so it's **back to the drawing board**."_       |
| **Keep an eye on**            | /kiːp ən aɪ ɒn/       | phrase    | Để mắt / Theo dõi              | _"Please **keep an eye on** the server logs."_                     |

## 💡 MẸO NÓI TỰ NHIÊN VỚI IDIOMS

- Không ép dùng idioms quá đà. Hãy dùng khi đúng hoàn cảnh.
- Khi làm việc với Tây hoặc đối tác: Cụm **"on the same page"** và **"get the ball rolling"** cực kỳ phổ biến trong các buổi họp Daily Standup!
