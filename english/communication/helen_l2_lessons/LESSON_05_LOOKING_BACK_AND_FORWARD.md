# 🎓 BUỔI 5 - LOOKING BACK & FORWARD

> **Level:** L2  
> **Chủ đề:** Looking Back & Forward - Quá khứ & Tương lai

---

## 📌 MỤC TIÊU BUỔI HỌC

- Làm chủ cách nói về các sự kiện trong quá khứ (**Past Simple**) và dự định tương lai (**Future Plans & Predictions**).
- Phân biệt các cụm trạng từ chỉ thời gian quá khứ (_yesterday, last week, 10 years ago..._) và tương lai (_tomorrow, next year, in 5 years..._).
- Ghi nhớ và phản xạ nhanh bảng **Động từ bất quy tắc (Irregular Verbs)** thông dụng nhất trong giao tiếp.

---

## 1. 🕰️ PAST QUESTIONS - CÂU HỎI VỀ QUÁ KHỨ (PAST SIMPLE)

### 📊 Bảng câu hỏi & Mẫu trả lời tham khảo:

| #   | Question                                                         | Model Answer                                                                                               | Grammar & Note                         |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **When was the last time you traveled? Where did you go?**       | The last time I traveled **was** 6 months ago. I **went** to Đà Lạt with my close friends.                 | `was` + past time, `went` (go -> went) |
| 2   | **What did you do last weekend?**                                | Last weekend, I **studied** TOEIC in the morning and **coded** my side project at home.                    | Past Simple regular (`V-ed`)           |
| 3   | **What was your dream job when you were a child?**               | When I **was** a child, my dream job **was** to become a pilot, but now I **am** a software engineer.      | `was` (childhood memory)               |
| 4   | **How much money did you spend yesterday? What did you buy?**    | I **spent** about 150,000 VND. I **bought** a cup of black coffee and lunch near my company.               | `spent` (spend), `bought` (buy)        |
| 5   | **When was the last time you were drunk?**                       | The last time I **was** drunk **was** at our company year-end party. I **drank** too much beer.            | `drank` (drink -> drank)               |
| 6   | **Have you ever thought about living in another country?**       | Yes, I **have thought** about living in Japan or Singapore for tech job opportunities.                     | Present Perfect (`have + V3`)          |
| 7   | **Show me a picture on your phone. Tell me what was happening.** | This **is** a photo of my team at work during our team building event. We **were having** dinner together. | Past Continuous (`were + V-ing`)       |

## 2. 🚀 FUTURE QUESTIONS - CÂU HỎI VỀ TƯƠNG LAI (FUTURE PLANS & PREDICTIONS)

### 📊 Bảng câu hỏi & Mẫu trả lời tham khảo:

| #   | Question                                                     | Model Answer                                                                                     | Grammar & Note                  |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| 1   | **What are your plans for next weekend?**                    | Next weekend, I **am going to** practice English speaking and hang out with friends in the city. | `be going to` (dự định sẵn)     |
| 2   | **Where would you like to live in the future?**              | In the future, I **would like to live** in a modern apartment in Da Nang or HCMC.                | `would like to + V` (mong muốn) |
| 3   | **Do you think humans will ever meet aliens?**               | Maybe! The universe is huge, so I **think** aliens **will** exist, but we haven't met them yet.  | `will + V` (dự đoán)            |
| 4   | **Do you ever go to fortune tellers?** _(thầy bói)_          | No, I don't. I **believe in** my own hard work rather than fortune tellers.                      | `fortune teller` = thầy bói     |
| 5   | **Would you ever travel to outer space?**                    | If it **is** safe and free, I **would love to** travel to space and see Earth from above.        | Conditional type 1/2            |
| 6   | **What is your dream for the future?**                       | My dream **is** to become a Senior Software Engineer and speak English fluently like a native.   | Goal / Dream expression         |
| 7   | **Do you think the future will be better than the present?** | Yes, I **believe** technology and AI **will make** our daily lives easier and better.            | Future prediction               |

## 3. 🗓️ TIME EXPRESSIONS - CỤM TRẠNG TỪ THỜI GIAN

```
PAST (Quá khứ)                   FUTURE (Tương lai)
------------------------------   ------------------------------
- Yesterday                      - Tomorrow
- Last week / Last month         - Next week / Next year
- 10 years ago                   - In 5 years / In 10 years
- This morning                   - Tonight
- Before I came to school today  - Before I die
- After I woke up this morning   - After class / After I retire
- When I was a child...          - When I retire...
```

## 4. 🔤 IRREGULAR VERBS - BẢNG ĐỘNG TỪ BẤT QUY TẮC PHỔ BIẾN

| Infinitive (Nguyên thể) | Past Simple (Quá khứ) | Meaning (Nghĩa)       | Example Sentence                             |
| ----------------------- | --------------------- | --------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Go**                  | **Went**              | Đi                    | I **went** to school by motorbike yesterday. |
| **Have**                | **Had**               | Có / Ăn               | We **had** lunch at 12:00 PM.                |
| **Come**                | **Came**              | Đến                   | My friend **came** to my house last night.   |
| **Eat**                 | **Ate**               | Ăn                    | I **ate** phở for breakfast this morning.    |
| **Drink**               | **Drank**             | Uống                  | He **drank** three cups of iced coffee.      |
| **Give**                | **Gave**              | Cho / Tặng            | She **gave** me a nice birthday present.     |
| **Begin**               | **Began**             | Bắt đầu               | The meeting **began** at 9:00 AM sharp.      |
| **Buy**                 | **Bought**            | Mua                   | I **bought** a new laptop last month.        |
| **Spend**               | **Spent**             | Tiêu (tiền/thời gian) | I **spent** 2 hours studying English today.  |
| **Get**                 | **Got**               | Nhận / Có được        | I **got** a message from my mentor.          |

## 💡 MẸO NÓI TỰ NHIÊN KHI HỎI ĐÁP QUÁ KHỨ - TƯƠNG LAI

1. **Dùng từ đệm duy trì hội thoại**:
   - _"Well, let me think..."_
   - _"Actually, 2 years ago..."_
   - _"To be honest, in the future..."_
2. **Kéo dài câu trả lời bằng nguyên nhân / cảm xúc**:
   - ❌ _"I went to Da Lat."_
   - ✅ _"I went to Da Lat last year because the weather there is cool and relaxing."_
