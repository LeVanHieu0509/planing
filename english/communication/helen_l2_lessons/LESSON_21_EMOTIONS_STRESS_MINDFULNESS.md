# 🎓 BUỔI 21 - EMOTIONS, STRESS & MINDFULNESS

> **Level:** L2  
> **Chủ đề:** Expressing Feelings, Handling Burnout, Empathy & Mindfulness - Bày tỏ cảm xúc, giải tỏa căng thẳng công việc, sự thấu cảm và lối sống tỉnh thức

---

## 📌 MỤC TIÊU BUỔI HỌC

- **Làm giàu vốn từ mô tả cảm xúc đa chiều**: Phấn khởi (`ecstatic`), kiệt sức do áp lực (`burned out`), lo âu bồn chồn (`anxious`), thanh thản (`at peace / serene`), biết ơn (`profoundly grateful`).
- **Nghệ thuật Lắng nghe & Thấu cảm (Active Listening & Empathy)**: `I completely understand how you feel`, `That must have been overwhelming`, `I'm in your corner`.
- **Kỹ thuật Quản trị Stress & Tỉnh thức (Mindfulness & Burnout Prevention)**: Kỹ thuật hít thở sâu (`diaphragmatic breathing`), viết nhật ký (`journaling`), ngắt kết nối phục hồi năng lượng (`unplug & recharge`).
- **Phát âm từ vựng cảm xúc chuẩn ngữ điệu & trọng âm**: *Overwhelmed, Resilience, Vulnerability, Compassion*.
- **Thực hành phản xạ 12 câu hỏi chia sẻ tâm lý & cảm xúc đời thực**.

## 1. 🧘 EMOTIONS & MINDFULNESS CONVERSATIONS - 12 CÂU HỎI & MẪU TRẢ LỜI 3 BƯỚC

### 🟢 Nhóm 1: Nhận diện & Bộc bạch Cảm xúc (Emotional Awareness)

| # | Question | Model Answer | Grammar & Phrasing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How are you genuinely feeling today deep down? | To be honest, I'm feeling quite grounded and energized after a restful night of sleep. | `feeling quite grounded` |
| 2 | What is an activity that always fills you with immense joy? | Immersing myself in a creative coding project or cooking a delicious meal for loved ones brings me pure joy. | Gerund subject: `Immersing` |
| 3 | How do you recognize when you are approaching mental burnout? | When I notice simple tasks feel overwhelming, my sleep gets disrupted, and I lose enthusiasm, I know burnout is knocking. | Metaphor / Complex sentence |
| 4 | How do you process feelings of disappointment or rejection? | I allow myself to feel the sadness for a day, write my thoughts down in a journal, and reframe the setback as a lesson. | `allow myself to` + verb |

---

### 🟡 Nhóm 2: Giải tỏa Stress & Thói quen Tỉnh thức (Stress Relief & Habits)

| # | Question | Model Answer | Grammar & Phrasing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | What is your immediate go-to ritual when anxiety strikes? | I practice the 4-7-8 breathing technique: inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 7, and exhale slowly for 8 seconds. | Colon for enumeration |
| 6 | Do you practice daily meditation or gratitude journaling? | Every evening before bed, I write down three specific small things I was grateful for during the day. | Frequency: `Every evening` |
| 7 | How does physical exercise help your mental health? | A 30-minute run releases endorphins that instantly wash away accumulated work stress and clear mental brain fog. | Causation: `releases... that` |
| 8 | How do you disconnect when working in high-pressure tech roles? | I set strict work boundary hours and take a 20-minute silent walk in nature without my smartphone. | `take a silent walk without` |

---

### 🔴 Nhóm 3: Sự Thấu cảm & Đồng hành cùng Bạn bè (Empathy & Support)

| # | Question | Model Answer | Grammar & Phrasing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | How do you comfort a close friend experiencing grief or failure? | I listen attentively without rushing to give unsolicited advice, reminding them that their feelings are completely valid. | `without rushing to give` |
| 10 | What does vulnerability mean to you in relationships? | Vulnerability means having the courage to show your authentic imperfections and fears without wearing a fake social mask. | `means having the courage to` |
| 11 | How do you handle feelings of jealousy or comparison on social media? | I remind myself that social media is a curated highlight reel, mute triggering accounts, and refocus on my personal journey. | `remind myself that` |
| 12 | What is a piece of life wisdom that keeps you grounded? | 'This too shall pass.' Recognizing that both extreme highs and painful lows are temporary gives me profound peace. | Wisdom quotation |

## 3. 🎭 EMPATHETIC SUPPORT ROLEPLAY (MẪU ĐÀM THOẠI LẮNG NGHE & ĐỒNG CẢM)

```
Friend: "Hieu, I honestly feel so exhausted. My manager rejected my project proposal, and I feel like an absolute failure."
You:    "Oh, I'm so sorry you're going through this. I can hear how much heart and energy you poured into that proposal. It must feel deeply discouraging."
Friend: "Yeah, it really hurt. I don't even know if I'm cut out for this job anymore."
You:    "Please take a deep breath. One rejected proposal does not define your immense talent or worth as a designer. You are one of the most dedicated people I know. Do you want to vent about it, or would you prefer a quiet walk to clear your head?"
Friend: "Thank you so much... Just hearing you say that makes a world of difference. Let's go for that walk."
```

## 4. 🔤 EMOTION & MINDFULNESS IDIOMS

```
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Thành ngữ bộc lộ cảm xúc và tinh thần tích cực.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
```

|---|---|---|
| **Over the moon** | Vui sướng ngập tràn | _"She was **over the moon** when she got the job offer."_ |
| **Burned out** | Kiệt sức vì làm việc quá tải | _"Working 70-hour weeks left him completely **burned out**."_ |
| **Keep your chin up** | Hãy lạc quan, vững vàng lên | _"**Keep your chin up**; every setback is a setup for a comeback!"_ |
| **In your corner** | Luôn bên cạnh ủng hộ bạn | _"No matter what happens, remember I'm always **in your corner**."_ |
| **Running on empty** | Cạn kiệt năng lượng | _"I need a full weekend of sleep; I've been **running on empty**."_ |

## 6. 💡 PRO SPEAKING STRATEGY - THE 3-STEP RESPONSE

- **Câu hỏi**: *"What is the most valuable lesson you have learned about emotional well-being?"*
- **Bước 1 (Direct Epiphany)**: *"The most liberating lesson I've learned is that it is completely okay not to be okay all the time."*
- **Bước 2 (Detailed Contrast)**: *"In our fast-paced society, we are constantly pressured to appear happy and hyper-productive. But suppressing negative feelings only makes them stronger."*
- **Bước 3 (Concluding Practice)**: *"Accepting sadness or fatigue with self-compassion is the true starting point for authentic peace of mind."*
