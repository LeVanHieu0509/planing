- **Làm chủ từ vựng về Môi trường & Khí hậu**: Lượng phát thải carbon (`carbon footprint`), năng lượng tái tạo (`renewable energy`), rác thải nhựa đại dương (`marine plastic debris`), hiệu ứng đảo nhiệt đô thị (`urban heat island effect`).
- **Thảo luận về Lối sống xanh & Tiêu dùng bền vững**: Giảm thiểu - Tái sử dụng - Tái chế (`Reduce, Reuse, Recycle`), nông nghiệp hữu cơ (`organic farming`), tiêu chuẩn ESG trong doanh nghiệp.
- **Thuyết trình về Giải pháp bảo vệ Hành tinh**: Chuyển đổi sang xe điện (`EV transition`), trồng rừng phủ xanh đô thị (`urban reforestation`).

## 1. 🌍 ENVIRONMENTAL CONVERSATIONS - 12 CÂU HỎI & MẪU TRẢ LỜI 3 BƯỚC

### 🟢 Nhóm 1: Thực trạng Biến đổi Khí hậu (Climate Reality)

| # | Question | Model Answer | Grammar & Phrasing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What visible impacts of climate change have you witnessed locally? | In recent years, our city has experienced prolonged scorching heatwaves and unprecedented flash floods during monsoon storms. | Present Perfect: `has experienced` |
| 2 | Why is single-use plastic such an urgent environmental crisis? | Single-use plastics take over 500 years to decompose, breaking down into microplastics that contaminate marine food chains. | Causation: `take... breaking down into` |
| 3 | What role does deforestation play in global warming? | Forests act as vital carbon sinks; destroying them releases stored carbon into the atmosphere and destroys wildlife biodiversity. | Metaphor: `carbon sinks` |
| 4 | How does air pollution affect urban quality of life? | Toxic PM2.5 particulate matter leads to chronic respiratory diseases and forces citizens to stay indoors with air purifiers. | `leads to... forces citizens to` |

---

### 🟡 Nhóm 2: Lối sống Xanh & Hành động Cá nhân (Green Habits & Eco-Living)

| # | Question | Model Answer | Grammar & Phrasing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | What daily habits have you adopted to reduce your carbon footprint? | I carry a stainless-steel water bottle, strictly avoid plastic straws, and commute via electric scooter or public transit. | Parallel actions: `carry, avoid, commute` |
| 6 | Do you think sorting household waste makes a genuine difference? | Absolutely! When communities sort organic compost from recyclable paper and metals, recycling plant efficiency surges significantly. | Conditional: `When... surges` |
| 7 | Would you consider switching to a plant-based or vegetarian diet? | I currently practice 'Meatless Mondays' to lower my personal environmental footprint without feeling overly restricted. | `practice [habit] to lower` |
| 8 | How can consumers combat fast fashion waste? | By buying high-quality timeless garments, repairing damaged clothes, and donating unneeded items to textile upcycling centers. | Gerund prepositional phrases: `By buying, repairing, donating` |

### 🔴 Nhóm 3: Trách nhiệm Doanh nghiệp & Tương lai Xanh (Corporate ESG & Policy)

| # | Question | Model Answer | Grammar & Phrasing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | Do individuals or large corporations bear greater climate responsibility? | While individual habits build cultural awareness, top industrial corporations and strict carbon regulations hold the true systemic leverage. | Contrast: `While X... Y holds leverage` |
| 10 | What is your opinion on transitioning to Electric Vehicles (EVs)? | EVs are a crucial step forward, provided the underlying electrical grid is powered by solar and wind rather than coal. | Subordinate condition: `provided that` |
| 11 | How can modern tech companies integrate green computing? | Tech giants must optimize data center cooling systems, run servers on 100% renewable energy, and design energy-efficient code algorithms. | Modal: `must optimize, run, design` |
| 12 | What gives you hope for the ecological future of our planet? | The unstoppable rise of youth climate activism, rapid drop in solar battery costs, and groundbreaking carbon capture technologies. | Nominal listing of positive trends |

## 6. 💡 PRO SPEAKING STRATEGY - THE 3-STEP RESPONSE

- **Câu hỏi**: *"What is the most pressing environmental threat facing humanity in the 21st century?"*
- **Bước 1 (Direct Thesis)**: *"The most existential threat is the combination of extreme climate volatility and accelerating biodiversity loss."*
- **Bước 2 (Detailed Interconnection)**: *"When ocean temperatures rise, coral ecosystems die off, triggering a catastrophic collapse of global fisheries that feed billions of people."*
- **Bước 3 (Inspiring Call to Action)**: *"Solving this requires international policy alignment, clean energy innovation, and a fundamental shift from mindless consumerism to sustainable stewardship."*
