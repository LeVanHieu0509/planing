## 🔤 GIAI ĐOẠN 1: PRESENT TENSES (S1-S2)

### 1.1 Simple Present - Thì Hiện Tại Đơn

```
CÔNG THỨC:
  (+) S + V(s/es)         → She works at TMA.
  (-) S + do/does + not + V → She does not work on Sunday.
  (?) Do/Does + S + V?    → Does she work at TMA?

KHI NÀO DÙNG:
  ✅ Sự thật, thói quen: The sun rises in the east.
  ✅ Lịch trình cố định: The train departs at 8 AM.
  ✅ Tần suất: I always arrive on time.

DẤU HIỆU NHẬN BIẾT (Signal Words):
  → always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never
  → every day/week/month/year
  → once/twice a week

QUY TẮC THÊM S/ES:
  → V + s: works, plays, runs
  → V đuôi -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o + es: watches, goes, fixes
  → V đuôi phụ âm + y → ies: studies, carries
  → V đuôi nguyên âm + y → s: plays, stays
```

**BẪY TOEIC Part 5:**

```
DẠNG 1: Chọn V đúng dạng
  "The marketing team ______ a meeting every Monday."
  (A) hold (B) holds (C) holding (D) held
  → Đáp án: (B) holds - "every Monday" = Simple Present, "team" = số ít

DẠNG 2: Lẫn lộn với Present Continuous
  "The company ______ in the technology sector."
  (A) specializes (B) is specializing (C) specialized (D) has specialized
  → Đáp án: (A) specializes - sự thật chung, không phải đang làm
```

### 1.2 Present Continuous - Thì Hiện Tại Tiếp Diễn

```
CÔNG THỨC:
  (+) S + am/is/are + V-ing → She is working now.
  (-) S + am/is/are + not + V-ing
  (?) Am/Is/Are + S + V-ing?

KHI NÀO DÙNG:
  ✅ Đang xảy ra ngay BÂY GIỜ: I am writing a report.
  ✅ Tạm thời: She is staying at a hotel this week.
  ✅ Kế hoạch gần: We are meeting the client tomorrow.

DẤU HIỆU: now, right now, at the moment, currently, today

KHÔNG DÙNG VỚI (Stative Verbs):
  ❌ know, believe, understand, want, need, prefer
  ❌ like, love, hate, own, belong, contain
  → I know the answer. (KHÔNG: I am knowing...)
```

### 1.3 Present Perfect - Thì Hiện Tại Hoàn Thành

```
CÔNG THỨC:
  (+) S + have/has + V3 (past participle)
  (-) S + have/has + not + V3
  (?) Have/Has + S + V3?

KHI NÀO DÙNG:
  ✅ Đã xảy ra, KHÔNG nói khi nào: I have visited Japan.
  ✅ Bắt đầu quá khứ → kéo dài đến hiện tại: She has worked here since 2020.
  ✅ Vừa mới xong: The manager has just approved the request.

DẤU HIỆU:
  → already, yet, just, recently, lately
  → since + mốc thời gian: since 2020, since Monday
  → for + khoảng thời gian: for 3 years, for a long time
  → ever, never (trong câu hỏi/phủ định)
  → so far, up to now, until now
```

**BẪY TOEIC:**

```
"The company has ______ its revenue by 30% since last year."
(A) increase (B) increased (C) increasing (D) increases
→ Đáp án: (B) increased - has + V3 (past participle)

"Ms. Park ______ in the sales department for five years."
(A) works (B) worked (C) has worked (D) is working
→ Đáp án: (C) has worked - "for five years" = Present Perfect
```

### 1.4 Present Perfect Continuous

```
CÔNG THỨC: S + have/has + been + V-ing

KHI NÀO DÙNG:
  ✅ Nhấn mạnh THỜI GIAN đang làm: I have been waiting for 2 hours.
  ✅ Hành động vừa dừng, còn kết quả: She has been crying (mắt đỏ).

DẤU HIỆU: for, since, all day, all morning, how long

SO SÁNH VỚI PRESENT PERFECT:
  "She has written 3 reports." → Nhấn mạnh SỐ LƯỢNG (3 reports)
  "She has been writing reports all day." → Nhấn mạnh THỜI GIAN (all day)
```

## 🔤 GIAI ĐOẠN 2: PAST TENSES (S3-S4)

### 2.1 Simple Past

```
CÔNG THỨC: S + V2 (past form) / did + not + V

DẤU HIỆU: yesterday, last week/month/year, ago, in 2020

ĐTBQT (20 từ PHẢI THUỘC):
  go→went, come→came, take→took, give→gave, make→made
  see→saw, get→got, know→knew, think→thought, say→said
  tell→told, find→found, leave→left, begin→began, write→wrote
  send→sent, spend→spent, pay→paid, buy→bought, sell→sold
```

### 2.2 Past Continuous

```
CÔNG THỨC: S + was/were + V-ing
DẤU HIỆU: at that time, while, when + Simple Past
DÙNG KHI: Hành động đang xảy ra thì bị CẮT NGANG

VÍ DỤ TOEIC:
  "While the manager ______ the report, the phone rang."
  (A) reviews (B) was reviewing (C) reviewed (D) has reviewed
  → (B) was reviewing - while + Past Continuous, rang = Simple Past
```

### 2.3 Past Perfect

```
CÔNG THỨC: S + had + V3
DẤU HIỆU: before, after, by the time, already (past context)
DÙNG KHI: Hành động xảy ra TRƯỚC 1 hành động khác trong quá khứ

VÍ DỤ: "By the time I arrived, the meeting had already started."
  → arrived (Simple Past) - had started (Past Perfect = xảy ra TRƯỚC)
```

### 2.4 Past Perfect Continuous

```
CÔNG THỨC: S + had been + V-ing
DẤU HIỆU: for + thời gian + before/when (past context)
DÙNG KHI: Nhấn mạnh thời gian LÀM trước 1 mốc quá khứ

VÍ DỤ: "She had been working for 3 hours before she took a break."
```

### ✅ BÀI TẬP S3-S4 (10 câu)

```
1. The company ______ (launch) a new product last month.
2. While I ______ (drive), I heard the news on the radio.
3. By the time we arrived, the presentation ______ (already/finish).
4. She ______ (work) at the bank for 5 years before she quit.
5. The technician ______ (repair) the printer yesterday.
6. They ______ (not/receive) our proposal before the deadline.
7. What ______ you ______ (do) when the power went out?
8. The flight ______ (depart) by the time we reached the airport.
9. He ______ (study) English for 2 years before moving abroad.
10. The manager ______ (approve) the budget last Friday.

ĐÁP ÁN:
  1. launched  2. was driving  3. had already finished
  4. had been working  5. repaired  6. had not received
  7. were...doing  8. had departed  9. had been studying
  10. approved
```

## 🔤 GIAI ĐOẠN 3: FUTURE TENSES + ADJ/ADV (S5-S6)

### 3.1 Future Tenses

```
WILL + V:
  → Quyết định tức thì: I'll help you with that.
  → Dự đoán: Sales will increase next quarter.
  → Lời hứa: We will deliver on time.
  DẤU HIỆU: tomorrow, next week, probably, I think

BE GOING TO + V:
  → Kế hoạch đã quyết định: We are going to hire 5 new staff.
  → Bằng chứng: Look at those clouds. It's going to rain.

WILL BE + V-ING (Future Continuous):
  → Đang làm tại 1 thời điểm tương lai: I will be attending the conference at 3 PM.

WILL HAVE + V3 (Future Perfect):
  → Hoàn thành trước mốc tương lai: By December, we will have completed the project.
  DẤU HIỆU: by + future time, by the end of
```

### 3.2 Adjective vs Adverb - SIÊU QUAN TRỌNG CHO PART 5

```
QUY TẮC CỐT LÕI:
  → ADJECTIVE (tính từ) → mô tả NOUN: a careful driver
  → ADVERB (trạng từ) → mô tả VERB, ADJ, ADV khác: drive carefully

CÁCH NHẬN BIẾT ADJECTIVE:
  → Đuôi: -ful (careful), -less (careless), -ous (dangerous)
  → Đuôi: -able (available), -ible (possible), -ive (effective)
  → Đuôi: -al (financial), -ent (different), -ant (important)

CÁCH NHẬN BIẾT ADVERB:
  → Thường thêm -ly: carefully, quickly, recently, significantly
  → NGOẠI LỆ: fast, hard, well, early, late (không thêm -ly)

BẪY TOEIC (RẤT HAY RA):
  "The project was completed ______."
  (A) success (B) successful (C) successfully (D) succeed
  → (C) successfully - mô tả verb "completed" → dùng adverb

  "The report provides a ______ analysis of the market."
  (A) comprehend (B) comprehensive (C) comprehensively (D) comprehension
  → (B) comprehensive - trước noun "analysis" → dùng adjective
```

### 3.3 LINKING VERBS - ĐỘNG TỪ NỐI (SIÊU QUAN TRỌNG)

```
★ SAU LINKING VERB → LUÔN LÀ TÍNH TỪ (KHÔNG PHẢI DANH TỪ HAY TRẠNG TỪ)

12 LINKING VERBS CẦN NHỚ:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
  NHÓM 1 - BE (phổ biến nhất):
    → is, am, are, was, were
    → "She IS happy." ✅ (không phải "happiness")

  NHÓM 2 - GIÁC QUAN (5 giác quan):
    → look    (trông)     → She LOOKS tired.
    → sound   (nghe)      → That SOUNDS interesting.
    → smell   (ngửi)      → The food SMELLS delicious.
    → taste   (nếm)       → This soup TASTES great.
    → feel    (cảm thấy)  → I FEEL confident.

  NHÓM 3 - TRẠNG THÁI:
    → seem    (có vẻ)     → He SEEMS nervous.
    → appear  (có vẻ)     → She APPEARS satisfied.
    → become  (trở nên)   → The market BECAME competitive.
    → remain  (vẫn)       → Prices REMAIN stable.
    → stay    (giữ)       → Please STAY calm.
    → get     (trở nên)   → It's GETTING cold.
    → grow    (trở nên)   → The company GREW successful.

BẪY TOEIC LINKING VERB (HAY RA):
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
  ❌ "The manager seems happily."    → SAI (trạng từ)
  ❌ "The manager seems happiness."  → SAI (danh từ)
  ✅ "The manager seems happy."      → ĐÚNG (tính từ)

  ❌ "The food smells wonderfully."  → SAI
  ✅ "The food smells wonderful."    → ĐÚNG

  "The results of the survey ______ encouraging."
  (A) remain (B) remaining (C) remained (D) remains
  → (A) remain - linking verb, "results" = số nhiều

PHÂN BIỆT: Linking Verb vs Action Verb
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
  LINKING:  "She LOOKS happy."    → looks = trông (trạng thái) → + adj
  ACTION:   "She LOOKS carefully." → looks = nhìn (hành động) → + adv

  CÁCH PHÂN BIỆT: Thay bằng "is/am/are"
    → "She IS happy." → OK → linking verb ✅
    → "She IS carefully." → SAI → action verb → dùng adverb
```

## 🔤 GIAI ĐOẠN 4: CONJUNCTIONS + CONDITIONALS (S8-S9)

### 4.1 Conjunctions - Liên từ

```
FANBOYS (Coordinating - nối 2 mệnh đề ngang hàng):
  For: vì      → She left early, for she was tired.
  And: và      → I study and work.
  Nor: cũng không → She doesn't sing, nor does she dance.
  But: nhưng   → It's expensive, but it's worth it.
  Or: hoặc     → Call me or send an email.
  Yet: nhưng   → He's young, yet he's experienced.
  So: nên      → It rained, so we stayed inside.

SUBORDINATING (nối mệnh đề phụ):
  Because/Since/As: vì
  Although/Even though/Though: mặc dù
  If/Unless: nếu/trừ khi
  When/While/Before/After: khi/trong khi/trước/sau khi
  Until: cho đến khi

TOEIC HAY HỎI - CONNECTORS:
  However = Tuy nhiên (→ đối lập)
  Therefore = Do đó (→ kết quả)
  Moreover/Furthermore = Hơn nữa (→ bổ sung)
  Nevertheless = Tuy nhiên (→ đối lập mạnh)
  In addition = Ngoài ra (→ bổ sung)
  Consequently = Kết quả là
  Meanwhile = Trong khi đó
```

### 4.2 Conditionals

```
TYPE 0 (Facts): If + Simple Present, Simple Present
  → If you heat water to 100°C, it boils.

TYPE 1 (Real/Future): If + Simple Present, will + V
  → If sales increase, we will hire more staff.

TYPE 2 (Unreal Present): If + Simple Past, would + V
  → If I had more time, I would study abroad.

TYPE 3 (Unreal Past): If + Past Perfect, would have + V3
  → If she had applied earlier, she would have gotten the job.
```

## 🔤 GIAI ĐOẠN 5: ADVANCED GRAMMAR (S10-S16)

### 5.1 Passive Voice - Câu bị động

```
CÔNG THỨC: S + be + V3 (+ by + agent)
  Active:  The company hired 50 employees.
  Passive: 50 employees were hired (by the company).

CÁC THÌ:
  Simple Present:  is/are + V3      → Reports are submitted weekly.
  Simple Past:     was/were + V3    → The building was renovated.
  Present Perfect: has/have been + V3 → The contract has been signed.
  Future:          will be + V3     → The order will be shipped tomorrow.

TOEIC BẪY:
  "All employees ______ to attend the training session."
  (A) require (B) requires (C) are required (D) requiring
  → (C) are required - bị động: nhân viên "được yêu cầu"
```

### 5.2 Relative Clauses - Mệnh đề quan hệ

```
WHO → người (chủ ngữ): The manager who approved the plan...
WHOM → người (tân ngữ): The candidate whom we interviewed...
WHICH → vật: The report which was submitted yesterday...
THAT → người hoặc vật: The company that offers the best price...
WHERE → nơi chốn: The office where we work...
WHOSE → sở hữu: The employee whose performance improved...
```

### 5.3 Word Forms - Dạng từ (CỰC KỲ QUAN TRỌNG)

```
NOUN (danh từ) - đứng sau a/an/the, sau tính từ:
  -tion: information, application, reservation
  -ment: management, development, agreement
  -ness: business, awareness, effectiveness
  -ity: quality, quantity, opportunity
  -ance/-ence: performance, experience, difference
  -er/-or: manager, director, investor

VERB (động từ) - đứng sau chủ ngữ:
  -ize: organize, specialize, customize
  -ate: evaluate, negotiate, participate
  -fy: identify, notify, modify

ADJECTIVE (tính từ) - đứng trước noun, sau be:
  -ful: successful, helpful, powerful
  -less: careless, useless, homeless
  -able/-ible: available, responsible, reliable
  -ive: effective, productive, competitive
  -ous: famous, dangerous, various
  -al: financial, professional, additional

ADVERB (trạng từ) - đứng trước/sau verb, trước adj:
  -ly: carefully, significantly, approximately

CÔNG THỨC NHẬN DẠNG NHANH:
  _____ + noun = ADJECTIVE
  verb + _____ = ADVERB
  the/a/an + _____ = NOUN
  S + _____ + O = VERB
```

**BÀI TẬP WORD FORM (10 câu - HAY RA NHẤT):**

```
1. The company announced a major ______ in its strategy. (CHANGE)
   → change (noun - sau "a major")

2. Customer satisfaction has ______ improved. (SIGNIFICANT)
   → significantly (adverb - mô tả verb "improved")

3. This product is highly ______. (COMPETE)
   → competitive (adjective - sau "is")

4. The ______ of the new policy will take effect next month. (IMPLEMENT)
   → implementation (noun - sau "The", trước "of")

5. We need to ______ our production process. (MODERN)
   → modernize (verb - sau "to")

6. Her ______ in project management is impressive. (EXPERT)
   → expertise (noun - trước "in")

7. The report was ______ written. (PROFESSION)
   → professionally (adverb - mô tả verb "written")

8. Please provide ______ information about the product. (ADD)
   → additional (adjective - trước noun "information")

9. The hotel offers ______ service to all guests. (EXCEPT)
   → exceptional (adjective - trước noun "service")

10. They ______ reviewed the contract before signing. (CARE)
    → carefully (adverb - mô tả verb "reviewed")
```
