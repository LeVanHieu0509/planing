**Invention:** The smartphone

## SCRIPT

**[Introduction]**

Hello everyone. My name is Tuan. Today, I would like to present an invention that I like the most. It is the **smartphone**.

In my opinion, the smartphone is one of the most important inventions in modern life because it has changed the way people communicate, study, work, and entertain themselves.

**[What The Invention Is]**

A smartphone is not only a mobile phone. It is also a small computer that we can carry in our pocket. With a smartphone, we can make calls, send messages, take photos, watch videos, use the Internet, study online, and manage our daily tasks.

Before smartphones became popular, people needed many different devices, such as a camera, a music player, a map, a notebook, and a computer. But now, all of these functions can be found in just one device.

**[Why I Like It]**

The first reason why I like smartphones is convenience. I can contact my family and friends anytime and anywhere. For example, when I need help or want to share something important, I can call or text immediately.

The second reason is education. I often use my smartphone to learn English, listen to TOEIC audio, search for new vocabulary, and watch useful videos. It helps me study more flexibly, even when I am not at home.

The third reason is productivity. A smartphone helps me check my schedule, take notes, set reminders, and manage my personal plans. It makes my daily life more organized.

**[Impact On Society]**

Smartphones have had a huge impact on society. They help people connect faster, businesses sell products online, students access knowledge more easily, and workers communicate more efficiently.

However, smartphones also have some disadvantages. If people use them too much, they may waste time, lose focus, or become addicted to social media. Therefore, I think we should use smartphones wisely and control our screen time.

**[Conclusion]**

In conclusion, the smartphone is my favorite invention because it is convenient, useful, and powerful. It supports communication, education, work, and entertainment. Although it has some negative effects, I believe that if we use it properly, the smartphone can make our lives much better.

Thank you for listening.
