# SCRIPT: ANNUAL EVENT PAIRWORK (Tiếng Anh Nâng Cao 2)

**Topic:** Recording your video in which you and your classmate take turns to ask and to answer about an annual event held in their hometown. Then invite each other to the coming events.

**Characters:**

- **A (You/Hieu):** From Da Nang (Event: Da Nang International Fireworks Festival - DIFF)
- **B (Classmate):** From Ho Chi Minh City (Event: Nguyen Hue Flower Street Festival)

_(Ghi chú: Bạn có thể đổi tên quê quán và sự kiện cho phù hợp với thực tế của bạn và bạn học)_

---

## 🎬 SCRIPT

**[Scene Start: A and B are casually chatting on a video call or sitting together]**

**A:** Hey Phát! Long time no see. How have you been?

**B:** Hi Tuấn! I'm doing great, thanks. Just been busy with assignments lately. How about you?

**A:** Same here! Hey, I was just thinking about my hometown, Da Nang, because summer is coming. Do you have any special annual events in your hometown?

**B:** Oh, definitely! I'm from Ho Chi Minh City, and the biggest annual event there is the **Nguyen Hue Flower Street Festival**. It happens every year during the Lunar New Year. Have you ever heard of it?

**A:** Yes, I've seen it on TV! It looks amazing. What exactly do people do there?

**B:** Well, the whole Nguyen Hue walking street is decorated with thousands of spectacular flower displays and stunning mascots representing the zodiac animal of the year. People dress up, usually in traditional Ao Dai, to walk around, take beautiful photos, and enjoy the festive spring atmosphere. It's really crowded but incredibly vibrant. What about Da Nang? What's the most famous event there?

**A:** In Da Nang, our most famous event is the **Da Nang International Fireworks Festival**, or DIFF. It usually takes place in the summer, around June and July.

**B:** Wow, an international fireworks festival! That sounds breathtaking. How does it work?

**A:** It's a competition between different countries. Every weekend for a month, two international teams will light up the sky over the Han River with spectacular fireworks synchronized to music. There are also street carnivals, food festivals, and live music performances all over the city. The whole city just comes alive!

**B:** That sounds absolutely fantastic. I love fireworks!

**A:** Actually, the festival is coming up in a couple of months. You should totally come to Da Nang this summer! I can be your tour guide, show you the best spots to watch the fireworks, and we can try all the local street food. What do you say?

**B:** That’s such a wonderful invitation, Tuấn! I would love to come. I'll definitely check my summer schedule. And hey, since you invited me, you must come to Ho Chi Minh City next Tet holiday! I’d love to take you to the Flower Street. We can take some awesome photos and try the special Tet dishes in the south.

**A:** That's a deal! I've always wanted to experience Tet in Saigon. I'll make sure to book my tickets early.

**B:** Perfect! Let's keep in touch and plan our trips. It was great catching up with you, Tuấn.

**A:** You too! See you in class tomorrow. Bye!

**B:** Bye!

**[Scene End]**

## 💡 Tips for Recording:

1. **Body Language:** Dùng tay diễn đạt, mỉm cười và giao tiếp bằng mắt (nhìn vào camera) để cuộc hội thoại tự nhiên.
2. **Pronunciation:** Chú ý nhấn âm ở các tính từ miêu tả: _specTACular, BREATHTaking, VIBRant, anTICipated_.
3. **Pacing:** Nói chậm rãi, rõ ràng, có những khoảng dừng nhỏ (pauses) giống như đang suy nghĩ thật. Không nên đọc giống như đang cầm giấy.
4. **Customization:** Bạn có thể đổi **Da Nang / DIFF** thành tên quê quán thật của bạn (VD: _Nha Trang - Sea Festival, Dalat - Flower Festival, Hanoi - Mid-Autumn Festival ở phố Hàng Mã_).
