> 🟢 _"Yes, absolutely. I've already adjusted my schedule for it."_

**Q2. "Do you have any time constraints or other commitments?"**
> 🟢 _"No major constraints."_
> 🟡 _"I've finished my previous Node.js project, and I'm handing over my remaining support work."_

> 🟢 _"I stay calm and break the work into small tasks."_
> 🟡 _"Clear priorities help me deliver on time without losing quality."_
> 🔵 _"If I see a risk, I raise it **early** - for example, 'Heads-up, this may take one more day because…' - so the team is never surprised."_

> 🟡 _"Production issues affect users directly, so response time matters."_
