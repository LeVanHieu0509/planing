## 📐 5 QUY TẮC NGỮ PHÁP CỐT LÕI - ÔN CUỐI

```
1. WORD FORM:
   VERB + ___      → ADVERB   (submit reports promptly)
   ___ + NOUN      → ADJECTIVE (a diagnostic fee)
   ___ + ADJ       → ADVERB   (relatively easy)
   Linking verb +  → ADJECTIVE (become popular)

2. CONJUNCTION vs PREPOSITION:
   despite / because of / due to  → + NOUN
   even though / because / although → + CLAUSE (S+V)
   ❌ despite the cost was high → ✅ despite the high cost

3. PARTICIPLE -ing/-ed:
   VẬT gây cảm xúc     → V-ing (interesting, boring)
   NGƯỜI cảm nhận CX   → V-ed  (interested, bored)

4. PASSIVE:
   Chủ ngữ BỊ tác động → be + V3 (was submitted / are required)

5. TIME CLAUSE:
   When/Before/After + PRESENT SIMPLE (không dùng will!)
   ❌ When he will arrive → ✅ When he arrives, we will start.
```
