## 1. CẤU TRÚC

| Dạng               | Công thức             | Ví dụ                      |
| ------------------ | --------------------- | -------------------------- |
| **(+) Khẳng định** | S + V(s/es)           | She **works** here.        |
| **(-) Phủ định**   | S + do/does + not + V | They **don't eat** meat.   |
| **(?) Câu hỏi**    | Do/Does + S + V?      | **Does** he **work** here? |

---

## 2. QUY TẮC THÊM -s / -es / -ies

| Trường hợp                    | Quy tắc         | Ví dụ                    |
| ----------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------ |
| Đa số                         | + **s**         | work → works             |
| Kết thúc -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o | + **es**        | wash → washes, go → goes |
| Kết thúc phụ âm + y           | đổi y → **ies** | study → studies          |
| Kết thúc nguyên âm + y        | + **s**         | play → plays             |

---

## 3. QUY TẮC VÀNG - KHÔNG BAO GIỜ QUÊN

> ✅ **He/She/It → V phải có s/es | Câu phủ định/hỏi dùng does → V nguyên mẫu**

```
❌ She doesn't works. → ✅ She doesn't work.
❌ Does he works?    → ✅ Does he work?
❌ She don't like.   → ✅ She doesn't like.
```

| Chủ ngữ                 | Câu (+)  | Câu (-) / (?)  |
| ----------------------- | -------- | -------------- |
| **I / You / We / They** | V        | do + not + V   |
| **He / She / It**       | V + s/es | does + not + V |

## 6. SO SÁNH VỚI PRESENT CONTINUOUS - TOEIC TRAP ⚡

|               | Present Simple                    | Present Continuous            |
| ------------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Dấu hiệu**  | always, usually, every day, often | now, right now, at the moment |
| **Ý nghĩa**   | Thói quen / Sự thật chung         | Đang xảy ra ngay lúc nói      |
| **Công thức** | S + V(s/es)                       | S + am/is/are + V-ing         |
| **Phủ định**  | don't / doesn't + V               | isn't / aren't + V-ing        |
| **Câu hỏi**   | Do / Does + S + V?                | Am / Is / Are + S + V-ing?    |

**Ví dụ TOEIC:**

```
She drinks coffee every morning.   → Present Simple (thói quen)
She is drinking coffee right now.  → Present Continuous (đang làm)

The restaurant serves breakfast.   → Present Simple (sự thật chung)
The waiter is serving table 5 now. → Present Continuous (đang xảy ra)
```

## 7. LỖI SAI HAY GẶP (từ bài tập 13/05/2026)

### ❌ LỖI LỚN NHẤT: Nhầm Present Simple → dùng is/are (5/20 câu sai vì lỗi này!)

```
❌ is studys         → ✅ studies       (every morning → Simple, không phải Continuous)
❌ Is opens          → ✅ opens         (every day → Simple)
❌ Is recommends     → ✅ recommends    (always → Simple)
❌ Do they eating?   → ✅ Are they eating? (eating → Continuous → dùng Are)
❌ does bring        → ✅ is bringing   (Look! right now → Continuous)
```

**Công thức phản xạ - nhìn dấu hiệu TRƯỚC khi chia động từ:**

```
Thấy: always / every / usually  →  Present Simple  →  V(s/es)
Thấy: now / look! / right now   →  Present Cont.   →  am/is/are + V-ing
```

### ❌ Lỗi 2: Dùng am/is/are thay vì do/does trong phủ định

```
❌ are not eat    → ✅ don't eat      (They → do not)
❌ I am not drink → ✅ I don't drink  (I → do not)
```

> **Nhớ:** Phủ định Present Simple = **don't / doesn't** - KHÔNG dùng am/is/are!

### ❌ Lỗi 3: Nhầm Does với Is trong câu hỏi

```
❌ Is the restaurant serve?   → ✅ Does the restaurant serve?
```

> **Nhớ:** Câu hỏi Present Simple = **Do/Does** - KHÔNG dùng Is/Are

### ❌ Lỗi 4: Quên -s/es với He/She/It

```
❌ She study every day. → ✅ She studies every day.
❌ She doesn't works.  → ✅ She doesn't work.  (sau does → V nguyên mẫu)
❌ Does he speaks?     → ✅ Does he speak?
```
