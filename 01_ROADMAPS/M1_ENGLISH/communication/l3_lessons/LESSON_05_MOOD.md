# 🎓 BUỔI 5 - MOOD 😃😡😴

> **Level:** L3 (SAIGON SPECIAL) · **Ngày học:** Thứ Ba 03/11/2026
> **Chủ đề:** Mood & Feelings - Nói về cảm xúc, tâm trạng; dùng **từ mạnh** thay cho "very + tính từ"

---

## 📌 MỤC TIÊU BUỔI HỌC

- **Hỏi & trả lời về tâm trạng tự nhiên** như người bản xứ: `How are you feeling?` · `I'm in a good/bad mood.`
- **Nâng cấp từ vựng cảm xúc**: thay `very good / very tired / very angry` bằng **1 từ mạnh** (`great`, `exhausted`, `furious`).
- **Phân biệt 2 dạng câu hỏi**: `How do you feel when…?` (chuyện thật, hay xảy ra) vs `How would you feel if…?` (giả định - câu điều kiện loại 2, đã học buổi 2).
- **Thực hành phản xạ 20 câu hỏi** trong handout theo công thức 3 bước.

---

## 1. 📐 GRAMMAR STRUCTURE - HỎI & TRẢ LỜI VỀ TÂM TRẠNG

### ❓ A. Mood là gì?

> **Mood** /muːd/ = _a temporary state of mind, or feeling_ - trạng thái cảm xúc **tạm thời**.

### 💬 B. Mẫu câu hỏi - trả lời

```
HỎI                                         TRẢ LỜI
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
"How are you feeling?"                      "I'm feeling + [tính từ]."
"How are you doing today?"                  "I'm in a good mood." (vui)
"What's your mood today?"                   "I'm in a bad mood." (không vui)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
⚠️ Dùng giới từ IN: "in a good mood" (❌ không nói "on a good mood")
```

### 🔀 C. Hai dạng câu hỏi trong bài

```
1) THẬT / THƯỜNG XẢY RA:   How do you feel when + S + V (hiện tại)?
   → "How do you feel when the WiFi doesn't work?"
   → Trả lời: "I feel annoyed." / "I get annoyed."

2) GIẢ ĐỊNH (Câu ĐK loại 2): How would you feel if + S + V2/ed?
   → "How would you feel if you found a wallet with 20 million VND?"
   → Trả lời: "I would feel shocked." / "I'd be over the moon."
```

## 2. 📈 TỪ THƯỜNG → TỪ MẠNH (EXTREME ADJECTIVES)

> 💡 Từ mạnh đã mang nghĩa "rất" → **không dùng "very"** phía trước (❌ _very exhausted_). Muốn nhấn thêm dùng **absolutely / totally**: _"I'm **absolutely exhausted**."_

| Thường | Mạnh hơn | Phiên âm IPA | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Good | **Great** | /ɡreɪt/ | rất tốt | _"I feel **great** after the gym."_ |
| Bad | **Terrible** | /ˈter.ə.bəl/ | rất tệ | _"I feel **terrible** when I can't sleep."_ |
| Scared | **Horrified** | /ˈhɒr.ɪ.faɪd/ | kinh hãi | _"I was **horrified** by the accident."_ |
| Angry | **Furious** | /ˈfjʊə.ri.əs/ | giận điên | _"My boss was **furious** about the bug."_ |
| Tired | **Exhausted** | /ɪɡˈzɔː.stɪd/ | kiệt sức | _"I'm **exhausted** after a long day."_ |
| Happy | **Amazing** / Thrilled | /əˈmeɪ.zɪŋ/ | tuyệt vời, cực vui | _"I feel **amazing** today!"_ |
| Sad | **Miserable** | /ˈmɪz.ər.ə.bəl/ | khổ sở | _"Rainy Mondays make me **miserable**."_ |
| Surprised | **Shocked** | /ʃɒkt/ | sốc | _"I was **shocked** by the news."_ |

## 3. 📚 VOCABULARY - TỪ CẢM XÚC MỞ RỘNG

| Từ vựng | Từ loại | Phiên âm IPA | Nghĩa | Ngữ cảnh |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Excited** | adj | /ɪkˈsaɪ.tɪd/ | háo hức | _"I'm **excited** about my new project."_ |
| **Annoyed** | adj | /əˈnɔɪd/ | bực mình, phiền | _"I get **annoyed** when the WiFi is slow."_ |
| **Anxious** | adj | /ˈæŋk.ʃəs/ | lo lắng, bồn chồn | _"I feel **anxious** before an exam."_ |
| **Energetic** | adj | /ˌen.əˈdʒet.ɪk/ | tràn năng lượng | _"I feel **energetic** in the morning."_ |
| **Jealous** | adj | /ˈdʒel.əs/ | ghen tị | _"I'm a bit **jealous** of his English."_ |
| **Embarrassed** | adj | /ɪmˈbær.əst/ | xấu hổ, ngượng | _"I was **embarrassed** when I forgot her name."_ |
| **In a good/bad mood** | phrase | /ɪn ə ɡʊd/bæd muːd/ | đang vui / không vui | _"Don't talk to him, he's **in a bad mood**."_ |
| **Over the moon** | idiom | /ˈəʊ.və ðə muːn/ | vui sướng tột độ | _"I'd be **over the moon** if I got 700+."_ |
| **Drive someone crazy** | idiom | /draɪv ˈkreɪ.zi/ | làm ai phát điên | _"Karaoke at midnight **drives me crazy**."_ |
| **Stuck in a traffic jam** | phrase | /stʌk ɪn ə ˈtræf.ɪk dʒæm/ | kẹt xe | _"I was **stuck in a traffic jam** for an hour."_ |

### ⚖️ Phân biệt: SHY vs EMBARRASSED vs EMBARRASSING

| Từ | Khi nào | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **Shy** /ʃaɪ/ | Ngại **trước / trong lúc** tiếp xúc - do **tính cách** nhút nhát | _"I'm **shy** when I talk to a beautiful girl."_ |
| **Embarrassed** /ɪmˈbær.əst/ | Ngượng, xấu hổ **sau khi một chuyện xảy ra** (lỡ làm sai, bị chú ý) - **cảm xúc của mình** | _"I was **embarrassed** after I called her the wrong name."_ |
| **Embarrassing** /ɪmˈbær.ə.sɪŋ/ | **Sự việc** gây xấu hổ | _"That was an **embarrassing** moment."_ |

> 💡 Quy tắc **-ed / -ing**: **-ed** = người cảm thấy (I'm embarrassed / bored / excited) · **-ing** = sự việc gây ra cảm giác đó (It's embarrassing / boring / exciting).

## 4. 💬 20 WORKSHEET QUESTIONS & MODEL ANSWERS

Công thức 3 bước: **Cảm xúc (từ mạnh) ➔ Lý do (because) ➔ Ví dụ / hệ quả (which means that / especially when)**

### 🅰️ HOW DO YOU FEEL WHEN…? (chuyện thật)

#### 1. …you are stuck in a traffic jam?
> _"I feel really **annoyed**, **especially when** I'm on my way to class. Saigon traffic at 6 p.m. can **drive me crazy**, so I always listen to English podcasts to make good use of the time."_
- **Phân tích:** annoyed ➔ hoàn cảnh cụ thể ➔ cách biến bực bội thành việc có ích.

#### 2. …you are stopped by the police?
> _"I feel a bit **anxious**, even when I've done nothing wrong. I just stay calm, show my documents, and cooperate politely."_

#### 3. …you drink too much?
> _"I feel **terrible** the next morning, **which means that** I can't focus at work. That's why I rarely drink more than two beers."_

#### 4. …you listen to a romantic song?
> _"It depends on my mood. If I'm in a good mood, I feel warm and happy. But if I'm missing someone, it makes me a little **miserable**."_

#### 5. …you can't sleep?
> _"I feel **exhausted** the next day, **especially when** I have to work with clients in the evening. That's why I keep a fixed sleep schedule."_

#### 6. …your neighbors sing karaoke?
> _"It **drives me crazy**! **Especially** at 10 p.m. on a weekday, when I need to concentrate on my work."_

#### 7. …you see a handsome man?
> _"Honestly, I feel a little **jealous**, haha! But it also motivates me to go to the gym more often."_

#### 8. …you see a beautiful woman?
> _"I feel **excited** but also a bit **embarrassed**, because I'm quite shy at first. I usually just smile."_

#### 9. …there is a thunderstorm?
> _"I feel relaxed if I'm at home - it's perfect weather for sleeping. But if I'm riding my motorbike, I feel **horrified**!"_

#### 10. …the WiFi doesn't work?
> _"I get really **annoyed**, **because** I'm a software engineer, so no WiFi means no work at all!"_

#### 11. …someone doesn't reply to your text message?
> _"I used to feel **anxious** and kept checking my phone. Now I stay calm, **because** people are busy, **which means that** I focus on my own work instead."_

#### 12. …you come to Tieng Anh Nghe Noi?
> _"I feel **energetic** and **excited**, **because** it's the only place where I can speak English with real people every week."_

### 🅱️ HOW WOULD YOU FEEL IF…? (giả định - câu ĐK loại 2)

#### 13. …you woke up tomorrow as the opposite sex?
> _"I would be **shocked** at first! Then I'd be curious to see how differently people treat me."_

#### 14. …your best friend told you that he/she was in love with you?
> _"I'd feel **surprised** and a bit **embarrassed**. I would be honest about my feelings, **because** I don't want to lose a good friendship."_

#### 15. …you found a wallet with 20 million VND?
> _"I'd feel **shocked**, but I would return it to the owner, **because** I'd feel **terrible** if I lost that much money myself."_

#### 16. …___ asked you to marry him/her?
> _"If it were the right person, I'd be **over the moon**! But I would want us to be financially and emotionally ready first."_

#### 17. …your boyfriend/girlfriend cheated on you?
> _"I would feel **furious** and **miserable**, but I'd walk away calmly. Trust is the foundation of any relationship."_

#### 18. …your doctor told you that you had one month to live?
> _"I'd be **horrified** at first. Then I would spend every day with my parents and do the things I always postponed."_

#### 19. …your parents didn't like the person you love?
> _"I'd feel **anxious** and sad. I'd listen to their reasons carefully, but the final decision would still be mine."_

#### 20. …you became fluent in English?
> _"I'd be **over the moon**! I could talk to foreign clients confidently, get promoted to senior, and maybe teach at a university one day."_

## 5. 🌟 BONUS: 5 CÂU HỎI ĐÀO SÂU (FOLLOW-UP)

```
1. "Why do you feel that way?"            ➔ Sao bạn lại thấy vậy?
2. "What do you usually do when you feel ___?"  ➔ Khi thấy ___ bạn thường làm gì?
3. "What puts you in a good mood?"        ➔ Điều gì làm bạn vui lên?
4. "When was the last time you felt ___?" ➔ Lần cuối bạn thấy ___ là khi nào?
5. "How do you calm down when you're angry?" ➔ Bạn làm sao để bình tĩnh khi giận?
```

## 6. 🔤 PRONUNCIATION - ÂM CUỐI "-ED"

```
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tính từ đuôi -ED đọc theo âm trước nó:
• /t/  sau âm vô thanh:   shocked /ʃɒkt/ · stressed /strest/
• /d/  sau âm hữu thanh:  annoyed /əˈnɔɪd/ · horrified /ˈhɒr.ɪ.faɪd/
• /ɪd/ sau t, d:          excited /ɪkˈsaɪ.tɪd/ · exhausted /ɪɡˈzɔː.stɪd/
⚠️ Nhấn âm cuối rõ ràng - bỏ âm cuối là người nghe không hiểu.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
```
