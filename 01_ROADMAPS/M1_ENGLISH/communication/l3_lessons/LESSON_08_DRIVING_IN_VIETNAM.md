# 🎓 BUỔI 8 - DRIVING IN VIETNAM 🛵🚦

> **Level:** L3 (SAIGON SPECIAL) · **Ngày học:** Thứ Năm 12/11/2026
> **Chủ đề:** Lái xe & giao thông ở Việt Nam - từ vựng đường phố, bị CSGT dừng xe, các bộ phận xe máy, 8 câu hỏi thảo luận

---

## 📌 MỤC TIÊU BUỔI HỌC

- **Kể về thói quen đi lại** (xe máy, ô tô, kẹt xe) bằng câu dài có lý do + ví dụ.
- **Dùng đúng cụm `get pulled over`** (bị CSGT dừng xe) và **8 lỗi vi phạm** phổ biến.
- **Gọi tên 13 bộ phận xe máy** - dùng được khi đi sửa xe / kể chuyện xe hỏng.
- **Trả lời 8 câu hỏi** theo công thức: **Câu ngắn + because + for example**.

## 1. 📚 VOCABULARY - ĐƯỜNG PHỐ & GIAO THÔNG

| Từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **street** | đường phố | _My street is always busy in the morning._ |
| **lane** | làn đường | _Motorbikes must stay in the right lane._ |
| **sidewalk** | vỉa hè | _People in Saigon drive on the sidewalk during traffic jams._ |
| **alley** | hẻm | _I live in a small alley in Go Vap._ |
| **intersection** | ngã tư | _Be careful at the intersection - people run red lights._ |
| **roundabout** | vòng xoay | _The roundabout near the airport is crazy at 6 p.m._ |
| **traffic jam** (n.) | kẹt xe | _I got stuck in a traffic jam for 45 minutes._ |
| **(to get) pulled over** (v.) | bị CSGT dừng xe / "thổi vào" | _I got pulled over for not using my turn signal._ |

> 💡 **Cụm hay đi kèm:** `get stuck in traffic` (bị kẹt xe) · `rush hour` (giờ cao điểm) · `cut in` (chen ngang) · `run a red light` (vượt đèn đỏ) · `give someone a ride` (chở ai đó).

## 2. 🚓 "DO YOU KNOW WHY I PULLED YOU OVER?" - 8 LỖI VI PHẠM

| Tiếng Anh | Nghĩa | Cách nói cả câu |
|---|---|---|
| **Speeding** | chạy quá tốc độ | _I got pulled over **for speeding**._ |
| **No helmet** | không đội mũ bảo hiểm | _He was **not wearing a helmet**._ |
| **No turn signal** | không bật xi-nhan | _I **didn't use my turn signal**._ |
| **No lights** | không bật đèn | _I was **driving without my lights on**._ |
| **Running a red (light)** | vượt đèn đỏ | _She **ran a red light**._ |
| **Driving in the wrong lane** | đi sai làn | _I was **in the wrong lane**._ |
| **Too many passengers** | chở quá số người | _He was **carrying too many passengers**._ (chở 3) |
| **Driving drunk** | lái xe khi say | _Never **drive drunk** - the fine is huge now._ |

> ⚠️ **Ngữ pháp:** _get pulled over **for** + V-ing / danh từ_ → _for speeding_, _for running a red light_, _for no helmet_.

## 4. 💬 8 QUESTIONS & MODEL ANSWERS

> ✏️ Câu trả lời mẫu theo hoàn cảnh của Hiếu - **chỗ nào trong [ ] thì sửa theo sự thật**.

**1. Do you know how to drive? Who taught you how to drive?**
> _"I can ride a motorbike, but I can't drive a car yet. **My [dad] taught me** when I was about [15] in my hometown, Nghe An. **For example**, he took me to an empty road every evening until I felt confident."_

**2. Do you always use a seatbelt when you travel by car? Why? / Why not?**
> _"Yes, I always **buckle up**, even in the back seat, **because** it can save your life in an accident. **Which means that** a few seconds of effort is totally worth it."_

**3. What is the legal driving age in Vietnam? When is the best age to learn how to drive?**
> _"It's **18** for cars and motorbikes over 50cc, and **16** for small bikes under 50cc. I think 18 is the best age, **because** people are more responsible and they can take the license test properly."_

**4. Would you prefer to drive a car or a motorcycle? What are the advantages / disadvantages?**
> _"In Saigon, I prefer a motorbike **because** it's cheaper and I can get through traffic jams faster. **The downside is** the rain and the pollution. A car is safer and more comfortable, **but** parking is expensive and you get stuck in traffic all the time."_

**5. Are women better drivers than men? Why? / Why not?**
> _"I don't think it depends on gender - it depends on the person. **In my experience**, women are often more careful, while some men drive too fast. **So** it's more about habits than gender."_

**6. Which city / district in Vietnam has the worst traffic?**
> _"Definitely Ho Chi Minh City, especially **District 1 and Tan Son Nhat area** during **rush hour**. **For instance**, the roundabout near the airport can take 30 minutes to get through at 6 p.m."_

**7. Have you ever been pulled over by the police? How did you feel?**
> _"[Yes, once.] I **got pulled over for** [not using my turn signal]. I felt **nervous and a bit embarrassed**, **but** the officer was polite, and I learned to be more careful."_
> _(Nếu chưa từng: "No, never - I always follow the rules, so I've been lucky so far.")_

**8. What rules would you break if you were late for work / school?** _(câu điều kiện loại 2 - buổi 2)_
> _"**If I were late**, I **might** ride on the sidewalk for a few meters to escape a traffic jam, **but I would never** run a red light, **because** it's too dangerous."_
