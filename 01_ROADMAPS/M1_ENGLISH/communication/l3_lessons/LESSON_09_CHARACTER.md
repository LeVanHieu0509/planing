---

## 📌 MỤC TIÊU BUỔI HỌC

- **Phân loại tính từ tính cách** thành ưu điểm / khuyết điểm.
- **Hỏi đúng ngữ pháp:** `Are you…?` (tính từ - cảm xúc, tính cách) vs `Do you…?` (động từ - hành động, ý kiến).
- **Nói 1 câu có lý do + ví dụ** với _should / shouldn't_.
- **Phản xạ hỏi đào sâu:** _Why? · Can you give me an example? · When? · Who with?_

## 1. 📚 VOCABULARY - QUALITIES vs FLAWS

### ✅ Qualities (ưu điểm)

| Từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **clever** | thông minh, khéo | _She's clever - she always finds a smart way to fix bugs._ |
| **generous** | hào phóng | _He's generous - he always pays for his friends._ |
| **outgoing** | hướng ngoại, cởi mở | _Outgoing people make friends easily._ |
| **optimistic** | lạc quan | _I try to stay optimistic when a project is late._ |
| **realistic** | thực tế | _I'm not pessimistic, I'm just realistic._ |
| **punctual** /ˈpʌŋk.tʃu.əl/ | đúng giờ | _He's never late - he's very punctual._ |
| **hardworking** | chăm chỉ | |
| **selfless** | vị tha, không ích kỷ | _Mothers are often selfless._ |
| **faithful / trustworthy** | chung thủy / đáng tin cậy | _A good friend should be trustworthy._ |
| **kind · friendly · loving · funny** | tốt bụng · thân thiện · giàu tình cảm · vui tính | |
| **patient** | kiên nhẫn | _A teacher should be patient._ |
| **reserved** | kín đáo, ít nói | trung tính - không xấu, chỉ là ít thể hiện |

### ❌ Flaws (khuyết điểm)

| Từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **arrogant** | kiêu ngạo (_to think you're better than others_) | _Arrogant people never admit their mistakes._ |
| **moody** | thất thường, dễ đổi tâm trạng | _He's moody - happy in the morning, angry in the afternoon._ |
| **pessimistic** | bi quan | ❌ ~~pesimistic~~ - 2 chữ **s** |
| **rude** | thô lỗ | _It's rude to check your phone during a meeting._ |
| **mean** | xấu tính, keo kiệt | |
| **impatient / short-tempered** | thiếu kiên nhẫn / nóng tính | hợp với _"gets angry when people cut in line"_ hơn **moody** |
| **lazy** | lười | _"always finds the easy way"_ có thể là **clever** hoặc **lazy** - tuỳ ngữ cảnh 😄 |

**Bài tập handout - đáp án:**
- **Qualities:** clever, outgoing, optimistic, punctual, selfless _(+ reserved: trung tính)_
- **Flaws:** arrogant, moody, pessimistic, rude

## 2. 📐 GRAMMAR - ARE YOU…? vs DO YOU…?

```
Are / Is / Am  +  TÍNH TỪ (tính cách, cảm xúc bên trong)
   → "Are you punctual?"        "Are you kind to other people?"

Do / Does      +  ĐỘNG TỪ (hành động, ý kiến)
   → "Do you get angry when people cut in line?"
   → "Do you spend more money on other people than yourself?"
   → "Do you usually try to find the easy way to do things?"
   → "Do you wake up feeling happy?"
```

> ⚠️ Lỗi hay gặp: ❌ _"Do you punctual?"_ → ✅ _"Are you punctual?"_ · ❌ _"Are you get angry…?"_ → ✅ _"Do you get angry…?"_

### 🔤 Sửa câu trong phiếu "Find someone who"

| Đã viết | Nên viết |
|---|---|
| prepare everything | _She **always prepares for** the worst._ |
| Long time to wait but other people behind me | _I **waited a long time**, and then someone **cut in front of me**._ |
| to resolve | _He always **finds a quick way to solve** problems._ |
| She is resolve the problem in her life | _She's **good at solving** problems in her life._ |
| make many friend in his life | _He **makes a lot of friends** easily._ |
| generus | **generous** |

## 3. 💼 "A ___ SHOULD / SHOULDN'T BE ___ BECAUSE ___"

> Mẫu handout: _"A teacher should be **patient** because students will be able to learn at a comfortable speed."_

| Người | Câu mẫu |
|---|---|
| a teacher | _A teacher **should be patient** because every student learns at a different speed._ |
| a good friend | _A good friend **should be trustworthy** because you need someone to keep your secrets._ |
| a policeman | _A policeman **shouldn't be rude** because people need to feel safe, not scared._ |
| an influencer / idol | _An influencer **should be honest** because many young people copy what they do._ |
| a businesswoman | _A businesswoman **should be confident** because she has to make big decisions every day._ |
| a husband / wife | _A husband **should be faithful** because trust is the base of a marriage._ |
| a salesperson | _A salesperson **should be outgoing** because they talk to strangers all day._ |
| a bartender | _A bartender **should be friendly** because customers come to relax and chat._ |
| **a software engineer** _(của Hiếu)_ | _A software engineer **should be patient** because debugging can take hours. **For example**, a bug in production once took me a whole day to find._ |

**Follow-up luôn hỏi:** _"Why?"_ · _"Can you give me an example?"_

## 4. 🎮 GAME "MARRY - KISS - KILL" (từ vựng mô tả người)

`rich` · `polite` · `handsome` · `confident` · `mean` · `dangerous` · `ugly` · `successful` · `gorgeous` · `good-looking` · `beautiful` · `fashionable` · `comfortable` · `energetic` · `strong`

> Mẫu: _"I'd **marry** someone **polite and successful**, I'd **kiss** someone **gorgeous**, and I'd **kill**… the **mean** one!"_ 😄
> 💡 **gorgeous** = đẹp lộng lẫy (mạnh hơn _beautiful_) · **good-looking** dùng được cho cả nam & nữ.

## 5. 💬 8 QUESTIONS & MODEL ANSWERS

> ✏️ Chỗ trong [ ] sửa theo đúng bản thân.

**1. What is your best character trait?**
> _"I think it's being **hardworking**. **For example**, I work two jobs and still study English three evenings a week."_

**2. Do you think many people change their personality at school or the workplace?**
> _"Yes. At work, people are often more **reserved** and polite. At school, they're more **outgoing** because they're with friends."_

**3. Did you choose your job because it matches your character?**
> _"Yes. I'm **patient** and I like solving problems, so being a software engineer fits me. Debugging is like solving a puzzle every day."_

**4. Do you have any character traits that are different from your family's?**
> _"[My parents are very **reserved**, but I'm more **outgoing** now] because I moved to Saigon and had to talk to many new people."_

**5. Which flaws are the most difficult to change? Which are the easiest?**
> _"**Arrogance** is the hardest, because arrogant people don't see their own problem. Being **late** is the easiest - you just need an alarm and a plan."_

**6. When / where do people's character change the most?**
> _"**When they move to a new city** or start a new job. **For example**, when I moved to Saigon, I became more independent."_

**7. Is there anything about your personality you are trying to change?**
> _"Yes, I'm trying to be **less of an overthinker**. I'm learning to make decisions faster and focus on action."_

**8. What character traits make someone a good friend, coworker, or leader?**
> _"A good friend should be **trustworthy**, a good coworker should be **punctual and helpful**, and a good leader should be **patient** and listen to the team."_
