## 1. 📚 12 NATIVE SPEAKER PHRASES

| # | Cụm từ | Phiên âm IPA | Nghĩa | Ví dụ trong handout | Khi nào dùng |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Play it by ear** | /pleɪ ɪt baɪ ɪər/ | tùy cơ ứng biến, chưa lên kế hoạch | _"What do you want to do tonight?" - "I don't know. Let's just **play it by ear**."_ | Chưa muốn chốt kế hoạch |
| 2 | **Do me a favor** | /duː miː ə ˈfeɪ.vər/ | giúp tôi một việc | _"Can you **do me a favor** and drop me off on your way home?" - "Sure, no problem!"_ | Nhờ vả lịch sự |
| 3 | **I'm dying to…** | /aɪm ˈdaɪ.ɪŋ tə/ | rất muốn, thèm được… | _"**I'm dying to** try that new restaurant." - "Me too! I heard it's amazing."_ | Diễn tả mong muốn mãnh liệt |
| 4 | **Never mind** | /ˈnev.ə maɪnd/ | thôi bỏ qua, không sao | _"I'm sorry, I can't understand what you're saying." - "**Never mind**. It's not important."_ | Bỏ qua chuyện không quan trọng |
| 5 | **I've got to go** (I've gotta go) | /aɪv ˈɡɒt.ə ɡəʊ/ | tôi phải đi rồi | _"Should we order another round?" - "No, sorry. **I've gotta go**."_ | Kết thúc cuộc gặp / cuộc gọi |
| 6 | **What's the matter?** | /wɒts ðə ˈmæt.ər/ | có chuyện gì vậy? | _"**What's the matter?**" - "I don't really want to talk about it."_ | Hỏi han khi ai đó có vẻ buồn |
| 7 | **No way!** | /nəʊ weɪ/ | không đời nào! / không thể nào! | _"There's **no way** you can eat 10 hột vịt lộn in one sitting!"_ · _"My girlfriend has been cheating on me with my brother!" - "**No way!**"_ | Phản đối mạnh **hoặc** ngạc nhiên tột độ |
| 8 | **Who cares?** | /huː keəz/ | ai thèm quan tâm? / kệ đi | _"I'm sad because my new picture on Facebook didn't get many likes." - "**Who cares?**"_ | Khuyên đừng bận tâm chuyện nhỏ |
| 9 | **I have no idea** | /aɪ hæv nəʊ aɪˈdɪə/ | tôi chịu, không biết | _"Why don't foreigners like durian?" - "**I have no idea**, durian is delicious!"_ | Hoàn toàn không biết |
| 10 | **Bottoms up!** | /ˈbɒt.əmz ʌp/ | cạn ly! (dzô!) | _"Today, our friend is getting married. Congratulations, buddy." - "**Bottoms up!**"_ | Nâng ly chúc mừng |
| 11 | **The more the merrier** | /ðə mɔː ðə ˈmer.i.ər/ | càng đông càng vui | _"Is it okay if I invite my friend to your party tonight?" - "Of course! **The more the merrier**."_ | Đồng ý cho thêm người tham gia |
| 12 | **Makes sense** | /meɪks sens/ | hợp lý, hiểu rồi | _"Sometimes English is difficult, though through tough, thorough thought, it **makes sense**."_ | Xác nhận đã hiểu / thấy hợp lý |

> ⚠️ **"No way!" có 2 nghĩa** - tùy giọng điệu: (1) **không đời nào** (từ chối) · (2) **không thể tin được!** (ngạc nhiên).
> 💼 **Dùng ở chỗ làm (NBCU):** _Makes sense · Do me a favor · I've got to go · Never mind_ - an toàn, lịch sự. **Tránh** _Who cares?_ với khách/sếp (dễ bị hiểu là thiếu tôn trọng).

### 🧑‍🏫 Ghi chú trên bảng của teacher (giải nghĩa bằng tiếng Anh đơn giản)

| Cụm từ | Teacher giải nghĩa |
|---|---|
| Play it by ear | Do something with no plan · = **Go with the flow** |
| Do me a favor | Help me |
| I'm dying to… | Something you really want to do |
| Never mind | It doesn't matter |
| I've got to go | I have to leave |
| What's the matter? | = What's wrong? / What is the problem? |
| No way | Can not believe |
| Who cares? | No one cares |
| I have no idea | I don't know 100% |
| Bottoms up | Drink 100% |
| The more the merrier | More people, more fun |
| Makes sense | Understand |

**Từ vựng thêm trên bảng:**
- **Exaggerate** /ɪɡˈzædʒ.ə.reɪt/ - nói quá, phóng đại (_"There's no way you can eat 10 hột vịt lộn"_ → câu nói phóng đại)
- **Colloquial English** /kəˈləʊ.kwi.əl/ - tiếng Anh khẩu ngữ, văn nói thân mật hằng ngày
- **Half** - một nửa (đối lập Bottoms up = uống 100%: _"Half!"_ = uống nửa ly thôi 😄)
- **I want to → I wanna** · **I've got to → I've gotta** (rút gọn khi nói nhanh)
- **What's up? / How's it going?** - chào hỏi thân mật (= How are you?)

### ➕ 12 CỤM TỪ BẢN XỨ BỔ SUNG (bảng thứ 2 của teacher)

| # | Cụm từ | Teacher giải nghĩa | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13 | **Long time no see** | Have not seen you in a long time | lâu rồi không gặp | _"Hey Quynh, **long time no see**!"_ |
| 14 | **No worries** | It's okay | không sao đâu | _"Sorry I'm late." - "**No worries**."_ |
| 15 | **Sounds good** | Agree with an idea | nghe được đấy, ok | _"Let's meet at 7?" - "**Sounds good**."_ |
| 16 | **I'm down** | I'm interested or willing | tôi tham gia, chơi luôn | _"Badminton this Saturday?" - "**I'm down!**"_ |
| 17 | **Fair enough** | I understand your opinion | cũng hợp lý (chấp nhận ý kiến) | _"I can't come, I have a deadline." - "**Fair enough**."_ |
| 18 | **My bad** | My mistake | lỗi của tôi | _"**My bad**, I sent the wrong file."_ |
| 19 | **You bet** | Of course / Definitely | chắc chắn rồi | _"Will you join the party?" - "**You bet!**"_ |
| 20 | **I'm starving** | Very hungry | đói meo | _"**I'm starving**, let's eat!"_ |
| 21 | **That sucks** | Something unfortunate / bad quality | tệ thật, xui ghê | _"My laptop broke." - "**That sucks**."_ |
| 22 | **Take your time** | Don't hurry | cứ từ từ | _"Sorry, give me a minute." - "**Take your time**."_ |
| 23 | **I'm on my way** | I'm coming | tôi đang tới | _"Where are you?" - "**I'm on my way!**"_ |
| 24 | **It's up to you** | You decide | tùy bạn | _"Pizza or phở?" - "**It's up to you**."_ |

> 💼 **Dùng với khách NBCU (an toàn, chuyên nghiệp):** No worries · Sounds good · Fair enough · Take your time · I'm on my way (vào call).
> ⚠️ **Chỉ dùng với bạn bè (thân mật):** I'm down · My bad · You bet · That sucks.

### ⚖️ Phân biệt: WHAT HAPPENED? vs WHAT'S WRONG? vs WHAT'S THE PROBLEM?

| Câu hỏi | Sắc thái | Khi nào dùng |
|---|---|---|
| **What happened?** | **Trung tính** - tò mò chuyện gì đã xảy ra (tin vui hay buồn đều được) | Bạn cười rạng rỡ / có chuyện lạ: _"You look so happy! What happened?"_ |
| **What's wrong?** / **What's the matter?** | **Tiêu cực nhưng quan tâm** - thấy người kia buồn, mệt, có vấn đề | _"You look tired. What's wrong?"_ |
| **What's the problem?** | **Tiêu cực, dễ nghe gắt** - nói nhanh/giọng mạnh nghe như trách móc | Cẩn thận! Ở chỗ làm nói mềm hơn: _"What's the issue?"_ · _"Is there anything I can help with?"_ |

## 2. 🎯 CÂU NÓI ĐÚNG NGỮ CẢNH - MINI DIALOGUES

```
👨‍💻 NBCU meeting:
   Client: "We'll deploy on Friday instead of Monday."
   Hieu:   "Okay, that makes sense. I'll update the plan."

👫 Rủ bạn đi chơi:
   Friend: "Can I bring my sister?"
   Hieu:   "Sure! The more the merrier."

🍜 Ăn uống:
   Friend: "Have you tried bún đậu mắm tôm?"
   Hieu:   "Not yet, but I'm dying to try it!"

📞 Kết thúc cuộc gọi:
   Hieu:   "Sorry, I've gotta go - my meeting starts in 5 minutes."
```

## 3. 💬 7 DISCUSSION QUESTIONS & MODEL ANSWERS

#### 1. When was the last time you did a favor for someone?
> _"Last week, a new teammate joined my team, so I **did him a favor** and helped him set up the project on his laptop. It took me an hour, but it **makes sense** to help new people get started quickly."_

#### 2. For what situations would you say "the more the merrier"?
> _"For parties, football matches, or eating hotpot with friends - **the more the merrier**, **because** everything is more fun with a big group."_

#### 3. For what situations would you NOT say "the more the merrier"?
> _"On a date, in a small meeting room, or when I need to focus on studying. Some things are better with fewer people."_

#### 4. Do you prefer to play things by ear or carefully plan everything?
> _"For work and money, I plan everything carefully. But for weekends and trips, I like to **play it by ear** - too much planning takes the fun away."_

#### 5. Traffic in Saigon doesn't make sense. Do you agree or disagree?
> _"I partly agree! Motorbikes go everywhere and nobody follows the rules, so it doesn't **make sense** to foreigners. But somehow it works, and locals know how to survive in it."_

#### 6. What are you dying to do in the future?
> _"**I'm dying to** speak English fluently and travel to Japan with my mom. I'm also **dying to** get my master's degree."_

#### 7. Is there any food that there is no way you will ever eat?
> _"**There's no way** I'll ever eat dog meat or insects. I'm open-minded about food, but those are my limits."_
