## 📌 MỤC TIÊU BUỔI HỌC

- **Làm chủ câu điều kiện loại 2** để nói về điều không có thật / tưởng tượng: `If + S + V2/ed, S + would + V`.
- **Phản xạ 13 câu hỏi "IF…"** trong handout bằng câu trả lời có lý do.
- **Thảo luận nhóm & thuyết phục**: chọn 5 món mang ra đảo hoang và giải thích **vì sao** (team discussion, giving reasons, agreeing/disagreeing).
- **Nối âm** `would you` /ˈwʊdʒ.uː/ và dạng rút gọn `I'd`.

---

## 1. 📐 GRAMMAR STRUCTURE - CÂU ĐIỀU KIỆN LOẠI 2

```
           IF + S + V2/ed (were / could / had),  S + WOULD + V-infinitive
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• If I had unlimited money, I would buy a house for my parents.
• If I could live anywhere, I would live in Saigon.
• If I were an animal, I would be a dog.          (⚠️ dùng WERE cho mọi ngôi)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Câu hỏi:  What would you do if + S + V2…?   ·   If you could…, where would you…?
Rút gọn:  I would → I'd   ·   I would not → I wouldn't
```

| Thành phần | Dùng khi | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **If + V2/ed** | điều kiện không có thật ở hiện tại | _If I **had** more time…_ |
| **If + could** | khả năng tưởng tượng | _If I **could** fly…_ |
| **If + were** | giả định về bản thân/trạng thái | _If I **were** you…_ |
| **would + V** | kết quả tưởng tượng | _…I **would** travel the world._ |

## 3. 💬 13 WORKSHEET QUESTIONS & MODEL ANSWERS

Công thức: **Trả lời trực tiếp (would) ➔ Lý do (because) ➔ Hệ quả / ví dụ (which means that / especially when)**

#### 1. What would you do if you had unlimited money?
> _"If I had unlimited money, I would buy a house for my parents in Nghe An first, **because** they have worked hard their whole lives. Then I would study abroad for my master's degree, **which means that** I could become a university lecturer later."_

#### 2. If you could travel anywhere in the world, where would you go?
> _"I would go to Japan, **because** I love how clean and organized it is, **especially** in spring with the cherry blossoms."_

#### 3. If you could live anywhere in the world, where would you live?
> _"Honestly, I would live in Ho Chi Minh City but spend Tet in my hometown. I love the energy of Saigon, **but** home is where my family is."_

#### 4. If you had to get a tattoo, where would you get it?
> _"I would get a small one on my inner arm, **because** it's easy to hide at work."_

#### 5. If you could have dinner with anyone in the world, who would it be?
> _"I would have dinner with my mom, just the two of us, **because** I live far from home and we rarely have time to talk deeply."_

#### 6. If you could only eat one food for the rest of your life, what would you choose?
> _"I would choose phở, **because** it's healthy, delicious, and I never get bored of it."_

#### 7. What would you do if you had no money buying groceries (left your wallet at home)?
> _"I would pay by QR code on my phone - in Vietnam almost every shop accepts it! If not, I'd ask the cashier to hold my items and go home quickly."_

#### 8. If you could have any superpower, which one would you choose?
> _"I would choose to speak every language fluently. **To be honest**, I'm studying for TOEIC right now, so that superpower would save me a lot of time!"_

#### 9. What would you do if you woke up tomorrow as the opposite sex?
> _"I'd be shocked at first, haha! Then I'd try to understand how differently women are treated at work and in daily life."_

#### 10. If you were an animal, what kind of animal would you be?
> _"I would be a dog, **because** dogs are loyal and always protect their family - like me."_

#### 11. What would you do if you were stranded on a desert island?
> _"I would build a shelter first, then find food and water, **which means that** we could survive until someone rescued us."_

#### 12. If you found a magic lamp, what three wishes would you make?
> _"First, good health for my parents. Second, enough money for my family to live comfortably. Third, to speak English fluently!"_

#### 13. If you could swap lives with one person for a day, who would it be?
> _"I would swap lives with a senior engineer at Google, **because** I want to see how they work and what makes them so productive."_

## 4. 🏝️ TRÒ CHƠI NHÓM: STRANDED

> **Tình huống:** Cả nhóm mắc kẹt trên đảo hoang **ít nhất 3 tháng** mới được cứu.
> **Trên đảo có sẵn:** nước ngọt (fresh water) · cá (fish) · dừa (coconut trees) · khỉ dữ (unfriendly monkeys).
> **Nhiệm vụ:** chọn **5 món** mang theo trong 12 món - _Choose carefully!_

**12 lựa chọn:** tent · matches · knife · rope · hunting rifle · flashlight · drugs & alcohol · toilet paper · fishing pole · net · first aid kit · condoms

### ✅ Nhóm Hiếu chọn & lý do

| # | Món | Lý do (nói bằng tiếng Anh) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🎣 **Fishing pole** | _"There's fish on the island, so we would never run out of food."_ |
| 2 | ⛺ **Tent** | _"We need shelter from the rain and the sun."_ |
| 3 | 🔦 **Flashlight** | _"It keeps us safe at night, especially from the monkeys."_ |
| 4 | 🔪 **Knife** | _"We can open coconuts, clean fish and build things."_ |
| 5 | 🔥 **Matches** | _"We need fire to cook the fish and keep warm."_ |

### 🗣️ Mẫu câu thảo luận nhóm

```
Đề xuất:      "I think we should take ___ because…"
              "What about ___? It would be useful for…"
Đồng ý:       "That's a good point." / "I totally agree."
Không đồng ý: "I see your point, but…" / "I'm not sure that's necessary because…"
Chốt:         "So, let's go with ___, ___ and ___."
```

## 5. 🔤 PRONUNCIATION - "WOULD YOU" & "I'D"

```
------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Would you  ➔  /ˈwʊdʒ.uː/  ("wud-joo")   - "What wud-joo do if…?"
• I would    ➔  I'd /aɪd/                  - "I'd go to Japan."
• Silent L:  would /wʊd/ · could /kʊd/ · should /ʃʊd/
• Desert /ˈdez.ət/ (đảo hoang)  ≠  Dessert /dɪˈzɜːt/ (tráng miệng)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
```
