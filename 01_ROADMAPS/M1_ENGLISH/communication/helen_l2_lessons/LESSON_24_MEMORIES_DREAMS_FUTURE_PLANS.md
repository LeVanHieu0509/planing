## 📌 MỤC TIÊU BUỔI HỌC

- **Kỹ năng Kể chuyện Quá khứ & Hoài niệm (Storytelling & Nostalgia)**: Kết hợp nhuần nhuyễn thì Quá khứ đơn (`Past Simple`), Quá khứ tiếp diễn (`Past Continuous`) và cấu trúc `used to / would`.
- **Thảo luận về Danh sách Ước mơ Cuộc đời (Bucket List & Adventures)**: Lặn ngắm rạn san hô Great Barrier, ngắm cực quang Aurora Borealis tại Na Uy, sáng lập dự án khởi nghiệp công nghệ.
- **Xây dựng & Thuyết trình Tầm nhìn 5 Năm (5-Year Career & Personal Vision)**: Tự do tài chính (`financial freedom`), thăng tiến vị trí quản lý kỹ thuật (`tech leadership`), cân bằng thân - tâm - trí (`holistic well-being`).
- **Tổng kết Hành trình 24 Chủ đề & Tự tin Phản xạ Nói Tiếng Anh Lưu Loát**.
- **Thực hành phản xạ 12 câu hỏi truyền cảm hứng sâu sắc nhất**.

## 1. ✨ DREAMS & MEMORIES CONVERSATIONS - 12 CÂU HỎI & MẪU TRẢ LỜI 3 BƯỚC

### 🟢 Nhóm 1: Kỷ niệm Đẹp & Kể chuyện Quá khứ (Cherished Memories)

| # | Question | Model Answer | Grammar & Phrasing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is a cherished childhood memory that always warms your heart? | I vividly remember carefree summer afternoons playing soccer in the rain with neighborhood kids until our mothers called us home for dinner. | `vividly remember` + V-ing |
| 2 | Who was the most influential mentor or teacher in your formative years? | My high school physics teacher inspired me deeply because he taught us to question everything and never fear making mistakes in experiments. | Relative clause: `because he taught us to` |
| 3 | What is a bold risk you took in the past that completely changed your life? | Choosing to pivot from electrical engineering into software development was a scary leap of faith, but it proved to be the best decision of my career. | Metaphor: `leap of faith` |
| 4 | How have your core personal values evolved over the past 5 years? | I've transitioned from chasing superficial achievements to prioritizing inner peace, deep relationships, and continuous mastery. | `transitioned from X to Y` |

## 6. 💡 PRO SPEAKING STRATEGY - THE 3-STEP RESPONSE

- **Câu hỏi**: *"What is the ultimate secret to achieving mastery in any challenging discipline like English or Coding?"*
- **Bước 1 (Direct Philosophy)**: *"The ultimate secret is understanding the exponential power of atomic daily habits."*
- **Bước 2 (Detailed Contrast)**: *"Most people rely on fleeting bursts of motivation and give up after two weeks. True masters build frictionless systems where they practice for just 30 minutes every single day, come rain or shine."*
- **Bước 3 (Inspiring Conclusion)**: *"Small, disciplined actions repeated consistently over years create results that look like overnight genius to the outside world."*
