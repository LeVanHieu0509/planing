# 🎓 BUỔI 1 - ABOUT ME

> **Level:** L2
> **Chủ đề:** Self-Introduction + Find Someone Who

---

## 📌 MỤC TIÊU BUỔI HỌC

- Tự giới thiệu bản thân bằng tiếng Anh (tên, công việc, sở thích, quê quán)
- Đặt câu hỏi và trả lời về bản thân người khác
- Luyện **Present Simple**, **Past Simple**, **Present Perfect** trong giao tiếp thực tế

## 1. ABOUT ME - 16 CÂU HỎI + MẪU TRẢ LỜI MẪU

### 🟢 Nhóm 1: Thông tin cơ bản (Present Simple)

| #   | Question                    | Model Answer                                                                         | Grammar               |
| --- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------- |
| 1   | What is your name?          | My name is **Alex**. You can call me **Al**.                                         | `be` + noun           |
| 2   | Where do you live?          | I **live** in Ho Chi Minh City, District 10. I **rent** a small apartment by myself. | Present Simple        |
| 3   | What do you do?             | I **am** a software engineer. I also **work** remotely for a tech company.           | Present Simple        |
| 5   | What is your favorite food? | My favorite food **is** chicken and pizza. I also **love** phở.                      | `be` / Present Simple |

> **💡 Mẹo nói tự nhiên:** Đừng chỉ trả lời 1 câu. Luôn thêm 1-2 câu chi tiết.
>
> - ❌ "I live in Ho Chi Minh City." (quá ngắn)
> - ✅ "I live in Ho Chi Minh City, near the central district. I've lived here for about 4 years."

---

### 🟡 Nhóm 2: Quá khứ (Past Simple)

| #   | Question                                       | Model Answer                                                                                                            | Grammar           |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| 4   | What did you do last weekend?                  | Last weekend, I **studied** for my TOEIC test in the morning, and I **played** football with my friends in the evening. | Past Simple: V-ed |
| 13  | What was your favorite thing to do as a child? | As a child, I **loved** playing video games and riding my bike around the village.                                      | Past Simple       |

> **⚠️ Past Simple - Động từ bất quy tắc cần nhớ:**
>
> - do → **did** | go → **went** | have → **had**
> - eat → **ate** | see → **saw** | take → **took**
> - make → **made** | come → **came** | get → **got**

---

### 🔴 Nhóm 3: Trải nghiệm (Present Perfect)

| #   | Question                                                   | Model Answer                                                                                         | Grammar             |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| 6   | What's one thing you've never done but really want to try? | I**'ve never** traveled to Japan, but I really **want to** try it someday.                           | have + never + V3   |
| 8   | Have you ever traveled abroad? Where?                      | No, I **haven't**. I**'ve never been** abroad, but I **hope to** visit Japan or Korea soon.          | Have you ever + V3? |
| 10  | How many countries have you visited?                       | I **haven't visited** any foreign countries yet, but I**'ve been to** many places in Vietnam.        | have + V3           |
| 14  | Where is the best place you've visited?                    | The best place I**'ve visited is** Đà Lạt. The weather **is** amazing and the food **is** delicious. | have + V3           |

> **⚠️ Present Perfect - Công thức:**
>
> - ✅ **S + have/has + V3 (past participle)**
> - ✅ "I **have visited** 3 countries." (kinh nghiệm, không nói khi nào)
> - ✅ "I **have never been** abroad." (chưa bao giờ)
> - ✅ "**Have you ever** traveled abroad?" (đã bao giờ...?)
>
> **So sánh Past Simple vs Present Perfect:**
>
> - "I **went** to Đà Lạt **last year**." → Past Simple (có thời gian cụ thể)
> - "I **have been** to Đà Lạt **3 times**." → Present Perfect (kinh nghiệm, không nói khi nào)

### 🎯 This or That - Quick Picks (trả lời nhanh)

| Question            | Model Answer            | Nói thêm                                          |
| ------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Coffee or Tea?      | I **prefer** coffee.    | I usually **drink** black coffee every morning.   |
| Beach or Mountains? | I **prefer** mountains. | I **love** the cool weather in Đà Lạt.            |
| Cats or Dogs?       | I **prefer** dogs.      | Dogs **are** more loyal and friendly.             |
| Netflix or YouTube? | I **prefer** YouTube.   | I **watch** coding tutorials and English lessons. |
| Talking or Texting? | I **prefer** texting.   | But I **want to** improve my speaking skills.     |

> **💡 "Prefer" vs "Like":**
>
> - "I **prefer** A **to** B." = Tôi thích A hơn B
> - "I **like** A **more than** B." = Tôi thích A hơn B
> - "I **would rather** V **than** V." = Tôi thà... hơn...

## 2. FIND SOMEONE WHO - GHI CHÚ TỪ LỚP HỌC

| Criteria                           | Who?                         | Cách hỏi (cần nhớ)                               |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| ...took a selfie today             | **Louis**                    | "**Did** you **take** a selfie today?"           |
| ...can speak more than 2 languages | **Ngọc, Văn**                | "**Can** you **speak** more than two languages?" |
| ...likes McDonald's                | **Vân** (Merle doesn't like) | "**Do** you **like** McDonald's?"                |
| ...can play a musical instrument   | **Sara, Merle** (saxophone)  | "**Can** you **play** a musical instrument?"     |
| ...smokes                          | Chị Vân **doesn't** smoke    | "**Do** you **smoke**?"                          |
| ...has studied at TANN before      | Chị An **doesn't**           | "**Have** you **studied** here before?"          |
| ...believes in ghosts              | **A Cúp**                    | "**Do** you **believe** in ghosts?"              |
| ...isn't from HCMC                 | **Merle** (Netherlands 🇳🇱)   | "**Are** you **from** HCMC?"                     |
| ...doesn't like phở                | **Hòa, Soup, May**           | "**Do** you **like** phở?"                       |
| ...has been to Cambodia            | **Ngọc**                     | "**Have** you **been** to Cambodia?"             |
| ...has never been on an airplane   | **Mỹ**                       | "**Have** you **ever been** on an airplane?"     |
| ...has dated a foreigner           | **An** (Italy 🇮🇹)            | "**Have** you **ever dated** a foreigner?"       |

> **📝 Cấu trúc câu hỏi theo loại:**
>
> - **Present Simple:** Do/Does + S + V? → "**Do** you **like** phở?"
> - **Can:** Can + S + V? → "**Can** you **play** guitar?"
> - **Present Perfect:** Have/Has + S + V3? → "**Have** you **been** to Cambodia?"
> - **Be:** Are/Is + S + ...? → "**Are** you **from** HCMC?"

## 4. NGỮ PHÁP CHÍNH - 3 THÌ CƠ BẢN

### 🕐 Present Simple (Thì Hiện Tại Đơn)

```
(+) I/You/We/They + V            | He/She/It + V-s/es
(-) I/You/We/They + don't + V    | He/She/It + doesn't + V
(?) Do + I/you/they + V?         | Does + he/she/it + V?

Dùng khi: Thói quen, sự thật, sở thích
→ I LIVE in HCMC. She WORKS at a bank. Do you LIKE phở?
```

### 🕑 Past Simple (Thì Quá Khứ Đơn)

```
(+) S + V-ed / V2 (bất quy tắc)
(-) S + didn't + V (nguyên thể)
(?) Did + S + V (nguyên thể)?

Dùng khi: Hành động đã kết thúc trong quá khứ
→ I STUDIED TOEIC last weekend. Did you TAKE a selfie today?
```

### 🕒 Present Perfect (Thì Hiện Tại Hoàn Thành)

```
(+) S + have/has + V3 (past participle)
(-) S + haven't/hasn't + V3
(?) Have/Has + S + V3?

Dùng khi: Kinh nghiệm (đã bao giờ), kết quả đến hiện tại
→ I HAVE VISITED Đà Lạt 3 times. Have you EVER BEEN abroad?

Signal words: ever, never, already, yet, just, before, so far, recently
```

> **🔴 Lỗi phổ biến của người Việt:**
>
> - ❌ "I have went to Đà Lạt." → ✅ "I have **been** to / **gone** to Đà Lạt."
> - ❌ "I have visit Japan." → ✅ "I have **visited** Japan."
> - ❌ "Did you ever been abroad?" → ✅ "**Have** you ever **been** abroad?"
> - ❌ "I am scare of ghosts." → ✅ "I am **scared** of ghosts."
> - ❌ "Chị Vân don't smoke." → ✅ "Chị Vân **doesn't** smoke." (she = doesn't)

## 6. 🎙️ BÀI TẬP LUYỆN NÓI (Làm tối nay!)

### Bài 1: Self-Introduction (2 phút)

Mở Voice Recorder trên điện thoại, nói liên tục 2 phút về bản thân:

```
"Hi, my name is Alex. I'm 25 years old.
I live in Ho Chi Minh City, in District 10.
I am a software engineer.
I also work remotely for a tech startup.
In my free time, I study for the TOEIC test and play football.
My favorite food is chicken and pizza.
I prefer coffee to tea. I drink black coffee every morning.
I have never traveled abroad, but I hope to visit Japan someday.
The best place I've visited in Vietnam is Đà Lạt.
I started learning English when I was in grade 3,
but I didn't take it seriously until this year.
That's why I'm practicing speaking English with ReflexAI now!"
```

### Bài 2: Hỏi - Đáp (tưởng tượng hỏi bạn cùng lớp)

Tự hỏi và trả lời thành tiếng:

```
Q: "Have you ever been to Cambodia?"
A: "No, I haven't. But my classmate Anna has been there."

Q: "Can you play a musical instrument?"
A: "No, I can't. But Merle can play the saxophone. She's from the Netherlands."

Q: "Do you like phở?"
A: "Yes, I love phở! But some of my classmates don't like it."
```

### Bài 3: Mô tả bạn cùng lớp

Nói về 1 người bạn biết từ buổi học:

```
"Today I met Merle. She is from the Netherlands.
She can play the saxophone. She doesn't like McDonald's.
I also met Anna. Anna can speak more than two languages.
She has been to Cambodia before."
```

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  PRESENT SIMPLE     → Thói quen/Sự thật                │
│  "I LIVE in HCMC."  "Do you LIKE phở?"                  │
│                                                         │
│  PAST SIMPLE        → Đã xong trong quá khứ            │
│  "I STUDIED TOEIC last weekend."                        │
│                                                         │
│  PRESENT PERFECT    → Kinh nghiệm / Đến hiện tại       │
│  "I HAVE VISITED Đà Lạt." "Have you EVER BEEN abroad?" │
│                                                         │
│  KEY PHRASES:                                           │
│  • I prefer A to B        (thích A hơn B)               │
│  • I am scared of...      (tôi sợ...)                   │
│  • I have never...        (tôi chưa bao giờ...)         │
│  • As a child, I loved... (hồi nhỏ, tôi thích...)      │
│  • I hope to... someday   (tôi hy vọng... một ngày)    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
