## 📌 MỤC TIÊU BUỔI HỌC

- **Làm chủ vốn từ vựng toàn diện về Trang phục & Phụ kiện (Wardrobe & Accessories)**: Nhớ và sử dụng chuẩn xác hơn 30 món đồ thường nhật từ đầu đến chân (`T-shirt`, `Pants/Trousers`, `Dress`, `Skirt`, `Blouse`, `Jacket`, `Coat`, `Tie`, `Necklace`, `Bracelet`, `Earrings`, `Sneakers`, `High heels`, `Flip-flops`...).
- **Nắm vững động từ & cụm từ chỉ thói quen ăn mặc**: `Wear - Wore - Worn`, `Dress up`, `(to) do the laundry`, `Rarely`, `Sense of fashion`.
- **Phân biệt cặp từ đối lập & cách dùng**: `Stylish` vs `Comfortable` vs `Uncomfortable`, `Casual` vs `Formal`, `Sneakers (US)` vs `Trainers (UK)`.
- **Làm chủ quy tắc phát âm chuẩn**: Phân biệt cặp phụ âm xì nhẹ **/s/** (*Suit, Socks, Sneakers, Stylish*) và âm xì môi tròn dày **/ʃ/** (*Fashion, Shirt, Shoes, Shopping*); phát âm đúng các từ hay sai như `Comfortable` (/ˈkʌm.fə.tə.bəl/ - 3 âm tiết) và `Clothes` (/kləʊz/).
- **Thực hành phản xạ 14 câu hỏi giao tiếp chủ đề Fashion** theo công thức 3 bước (Direct Answer ➔ Reason/Detail ➔ Personal Example).

## 3. 💬 14 SPEAKING QUESTIONS & 3-STEP MODEL ANSWERS

Dưới đây là 14 câu hỏi chính trong phiếu bài học và câu trả lời chuẩn 3 bước (*Direct Answer ➔ Detailed Reason ➔ Personal Example*):

---

### 1. What are you wearing?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"Right now, I am wearing a dark blue cotton **T-shirt** paired with comfortable black **pants** and an Apple **Watch** on my wrist. Since the weather in Ho Chi Minh City is quite warm today, I chose lightweight and breathable fabrics to stay cool while working."_
- **Phân tích:** Dùng thì Hiện tại tiếp diễn (`am wearing`), liệt kê trang phục từ trên xuống dưới kèm chất liệu (`cotton`, `breathable`).

---

### 2. Do you like to go shopping for clothes? Where is your favorite place to find new clothes?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"Yes, I really enjoy shopping for clothes, especially when I want to refresh my wardrobe. My favorite places to shop are popular local stores in District 1 and shopping malls like Takashimaya, because I prefer trying on clothes directly to ensure a perfect fit."_
- **Phân tích:** Trả lời trực tiếp (`Yes, I enjoy...`) ➔ Nêu địa điểm cụ thể (`Takashimaya, local stores`) ➔ Lý do (`ensure a perfect fit`).

---

### 3. How often do you dress up? How do you feel when you dress up?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"I rarely dress up on regular weekdays because my software engineering job has a relaxed dress code. However, I always **dress up** for weddings, client meetings, or special dinner dates. When I put on a sharp blazer and formal leather shoes, I feel much more confident, sophisticated, and energized."_
- **Phân tích:** Dùng trạng từ chỉ tần suất (`rarely on weekdays`) ➔ Thời điểm diện đồ (`weddings, dinner dates`) ➔ Cảm xúc (`confident, sophisticated`).

---

### 4. What do you usually wear when you are relaxing at home?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"When relaxing at home, comfort is my top priority. I usually wear an oversized cotton T-shirt and loose **boxer shorts** or sweatpants, along with a soft pair of **slippers**. It allows me to unwind completely after long hours sitting at the desk."_
- **Phân tích:** Nêu nguyên tắc (`comfort is top priority`) ➔ Liệt kê trang phục mặc nhà (`loose boxer shorts, slippers`).

---

### 5. What do you usually wear when you go to the beach?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"When I go to the beach in Vung Tau or Da Nang, I typically wear floral swim trunks, a breezy linen shirt, **flip-flops**, and polarized **sunglasses**. I also make sure to bring swim **goggles** whenever I go for a deep swim in the ocean."_
- **Phân tích:** Trang phục đi biển chuẩn (`swim trunks, linen shirt, flip-flops, sunglasses, goggles`).

---

### 6. What do you wear when you sleep?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"I generally sleep in very lightweight loungewear, like thin cotton shorts and a soft tank top. In the hot summer months, I prefer sleeping with minimal clothing because it helps me maintain a cooler body temperature and sleep much deeper."_
- **Phân tích:** Miêu tả trang phục ngủ đơn giản, thoáng khí (`lightweight loungewear, minimal clothing`).

---

### 7. How often do you do the laundry?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"I usually **do the laundry** about twice a week, typically on Wednesday evening and Sunday morning. I always separate white shirts from colored clothes to prevent color bleeding, and I use a fresh scented fabric softener."_
- **Phân tích:** Tần suất rõ ràng (`twice a week`) ➔ Thói quen phân loại đồ (`separate whites from colored clothes`).

---

### 8. Which do you prefer: stylish clothes or comfortable clothes?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"If I had to choose, I definitely prioritize **comfortable clothes** for everyday wear. As a developer, I spend 8 to 10 hours daily coding, so stiff or tight clothes would drive me crazy. However, for special events, I believe in balancing comfort with a stylish, polished silhouette."_
- **Phân tích:** Đưa ra lựa chọn dứt khoát ➔ Giải thích gắn liền với tính chất công việc ➔ Nêu góc nhìn cân bằng (`balance comfort with style`).

---

### 9. Do you like to wear jewelry? What do you think about people who wear a lot of jewelry?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"I personally prefer minimalist jewelry — just a sleek titanium wristwatch and occasionally a simple silver **bracelet**. Regarding people who wear heavy gold **necklaces**, rings, and multiple **piercings**, I think it's a bold form of personal self-expression, as long as it aligns with their style and situation."_
- **Phân tích:** Gu cá nhân (`minimalist jewelry: watch, bracelet`) ➔ Quan điểm khách quan về người đeo nhiều trang sức (`bold self-expression`).

---

### 10. What do you think about women who wear high heels?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"I think women look remarkably elegant, poised, and confident when wearing **high heels**, as they enhance posture significantly. However, I also deeply respect how physically demanding they are, so I admire women who wear them gracefully throughout long events."_
- **Phân tích:** Đánh giá mặt thẩm mỹ (`elegant, poised, enhance posture`) ➔ Sự thấu hiểu về độ mỏi chân (`physically demanding`).

---

### 11. What do you think about men who wear suits?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"A well-fitted **suit** instantly gives a man a professional, mature, and charismatic appearance. In business and formal galas, wearing a tailored suit with a matching **tie** commands respect and shows great courtesy toward everyone in the room."_
- **Phân tích:** Từ vựng nâng cao: `well-fitted suit`, `charismatic appearance`, `commands respect`, `courtesy`.

---

### 12. What do you like to wear on a night out?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"For a Friday night out with friends at a rooftop pub or lounge, I usually opt for a smart-casual look: a tailored black polo shirt, slim-fit dark jeans, clean white **sneakers**, and my favorite leather **watch**."_
- **Phân tích:** Phong cách `smart-casual` chuẩn chỉ cho buổi tối: `black polo, slim-fit jeans, white sneakers, leather watch`.

---

### 13. What colors do you think look good on you?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"I think neutral and cool tones look best on me, particularly navy blue, charcoal gray, and crisp white. These colors complement my skin tone well, create a clean modern aesthetic, and are effortlessly easy to mix and match."_
- **Phân tích:** Tông màu trung tính: `navy blue, charcoal gray, crisp white` ➔ Lý do (`complement skin tone, easy to mix and match`).

---

### 14. Which of your teachers has a better sense of fashion?
- **Model Answer:**  
  _"Among my teachers, I think Teacher Jake has an outstanding **sense of fashion**. He always wears sharp, well-coordinated shirts with stylish accessories, which creates a very vibrant, modern, and inspiring classroom energy."_
- **Phân tích:** Khen ngợi tự nhiên, dùng từ vựng: `outstanding sense of fashion`, `well-coordinated`, `inspiring classroom energy`.

## 6. 🔤 SPECIAL PRONUNCIATION: /s/ VS /ʃ/ & COMMON PITFALLS

```
------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Âm /s/ (Xì nhẹ, đầu lưỡi sát răng trên)  : Suit, Socks, Sneakers, Stylish, Silver
• Âm /ʃ/ (Chu tròn môi, xì hơi dày)         : Fashion, Shirt, Shoes, Shopping, Short
------------------------------------------------------------------------
```

| Từ vựng | Phiên âm chuẩn | Lỗi sai phổ biến cần tránh |
|---|---|---|
| **Comfortable** | /ˈkʌm.fə.tə.bəl/ | ❌ Đọc 4 âm "com-for-ta-ble" ➔ ✅ Đọc 3 âm **"KUMF-tuh-buhl"** |
| **Clothes** | /kləʊðz/ hoặc /kləʊz/ | ❌ Đọc là "cờ-lô-thét" ➔ ✅ Đọc giống từ **"close"** |

| **Suit** | /suːt/ / /sjuːt/ | ❌ Đọc là "suỵt" (/ʃuːt/) ➔ ✅ Đọc /s/ thẳng môi: **"soot"** |
