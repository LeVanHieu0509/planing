# 🎓 BUỔI 17 - OFFICE COMMUNICATION & MEETINGS

> **Level:** L3  
> **Chủ đề:** Daily Standups, Sprint Planning, Giving Constructive Feedback & Email Etiquette - Giao tiếp công sở, họp Agile/Standup, thảo luận tiến độ, đóng góp ý kiến và văn hóa email chuyên nghiệp

---

## 📌 MỤC TIÊU BUỔI HỌC

- **Báo cáo tiến độ họp Daily Standup 3 bước chuẩn Scrum**: Đã làm gì (`what I accomplished`), sẽ làm gì hôm nay (`what I'm tackling today`), khó khăn (`impediments / blockers`).
- **Nghệ thuật ngắt lời & Đóng góp ý kiến văn minh**: Cách xin phát biểu khéo léo trong cuộc họp (`chime in`, `add my two cents`, `interrupt politely`).
- **Phản hồi mang tính xây dựng (Constructive Feedback)**: Đưa ra lời khuyên cho đồng nghiệp theo phương pháp Sandwich Feedback mà không gây mất lòng.
- **Viết Email & Tin nhắn Slack chuẩn quốc tế**: Tiêu đề rõ ràng (`subject line`), hạn chót (`deadline`, `EOD`, `ASAP`), văn phong trang trọng vừa phải.
- **Thực hành phản xạ 12 tình huống giao tiếp văn phòng thực tế**.

## 1. 🏢 OFFICE CONVERSATIONS - 12 CÂU HỎI & MẪU TRẢ LỜI 3 BƯỚC

### 🟢 Nhóm 1: Daily Standup & Báo cáo Tiến độ (Scrum Updates)

| # | Question | Model Answer | Grammar & Phrasing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What did you work on yesterday? | Yesterday, I finalized the API rate-limiting feature and merged the pull request after peer review. | Past Simple: `finalized / merged` |
| 2 | What are your main focuses for today? | Today, I will be writing end-to-end integration tests for the authentication module. | Future Continuous: `will be writing` |
| 3 | Do you have any blockers or impediments? | I'm currently blocked on the third-party payment webhook documentation; I'll sync with Tom after standup. | `blocked on` + noun |
| 4 | How confident are you in hitting this sprint deadline? | We are tracking well on schedule and expect to deliver all core features before Friday 4 PM. | `tracking on schedule` |

---

### 🟡 Nhóm 2: Thảo luận & Đóng góp Ý kiến trong Họp (Meeting Interactions)

| # | Question | Model Answer | Grammar & Phrasing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | May I quickly chime in on this architecture proposal? | Absolutely! We'd love to hear your insights regarding database indexing. | Idiom: `chime in` |
| 6 | Could you clarify the acceptance criteria for this user story? | Certainly! The user must receive an instant confirmation SMS within 3 seconds of transaction completion. | Modal: `must receive` |
| 7 | How should we resolve this technical tradeoff? | Let's weigh the development speed against future maintainability before making a final executive decision. | `weigh A against B` |
| 8 | Can we table this discussion for an offline 1-on-1? | Good idea! Let's take this offline so we don't hold up the rest of the team. | Idioms: `table / take offline` |

---

### 🔴 Nhóm 3: Phản hồi & Xử lý Khủng hoảng Công sở (Feedback & Conflicts)

| # | Question | Model Answer | Grammar & Phrasing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | How do you deliver constructive criticism to a teammate? | I praise their dedication first, pinpoint the specific technical bug objectively, and offer to pair-program on a fix. | `Sandwich feedback` technique |
| 10 | What do you do when a colleague misses their commitment? | I reach out privately to understand if they ran into unexpected hurdles and see how we can redistribute workload. | `reach out privately` |
| 11 | How do you handle scope creep from clients? | I politely explain that new features will impact the timeline and propose adding them to Phase 2 backlog. | `scope creep` / `backlog` |
| 12 | What is your protocol for reporting an emergency server outage? | I post an alert in the incident channel, trigger on-call escalation, and post live status updates every 15 minutes. | Protocol verbs in Present Simple |

## 2. 🎯 THIS OR THAT - QUICK PICKS (PHẢN XẠ NHANH)

| Question | Model Answer | Giải thích thêm |
|---|---|---|
| **Morning meeting or Afternoon meeting?** | I prefer **morning meetings**. | Getting team alignments out of the way early leaves the entire afternoon open for uninterrupted deep focus coding. |
| **Slack message or Quick huddle call?** | I prefer **quick 3-minute huddles**. | Resolving complex logic over voice saves 30 minutes of back-and-forth typing and prevents misunderstandings. |
| **Camera ON or Camera OFF during remote calls?** | I prefer **camera ON**. | Seeing facial expressions and body language builds human trust and makes virtual collaboration far more engaging. |
| **Agile Scrum or Kanban board?** | I prefer **Agile Scrum**. | 2-week timeboxed sprints give the team clear goalposts and a structured cadence for continuous improvement. |

## 3. 🎭 SPRINT PLANNING ROLEPLAY (MẪU TRANH BIỆN TÍNH NĂNG TRONG SPRINT)

```
Scrum Master: "Team, we have 40 story points in the backlog. Should we include the Dark Mode feature in this sprint?"
Lead Dev:     "From an engineering standpoint, our priority should be refactoring the database schema to fix server lag."
You:          "May I add my two cents here? I agree with the Lead Dev. If our backend is sluggish, users won't care about Dark Mode. Let's fix performance first and push Dark Mode to next sprint."
Product Owner:"That makes total sense. Let's prioritize the database refactoring and commit to 30 points this sprint."
Scrum Master: "Agreed. Let's lock the sprint backlog and start coding!"
```

## 4. 🔤 CORPORATE IDIOMS & PHRASAL VERBS

```
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Thuật ngữ giao tiếp văn phòng quốc tế giúp bạn hòa nhập tức thì với sếp và đồng nghiệp nước ngoài.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
```

| Cụm từ / Thành ngữ | Nghĩa thực tế | Ví dụ áp dụng |
|---|---|---|
| **Touch base** | Trao đổi nhanh, cập nhật | _"Let's **touch base** after the client demo at 3 PM."_ |
| **On the same page** | Cùng chung quan điểm / Hiểu rõ | _"I want to make sure the designers and devs are **on the same page**."_ |
| **Take it offline** | Trao đổi riêng sau cuộc họp | _"This is a detailed niche issue; let's **take it offline**."_ |
| **Wrap up** | Kết thúc, tóm tắt cuộc họp | _"Let's **wrap up** today's meeting and summarize action items."_ |
| **Bandwidth** | Thời gian & năng lượng làm việc | _"I'd love to help, but I don't have enough **bandwidth** this sprint."_ |

## 6. 💡 PRO SPEAKING STRATEGY - THE 3-STEP RESPONSE

- **Câu hỏi**: *"How do you handle high pressure when approaching a critical product release?"*
- **Bước 1 (Direct Mindset)**: *"I stay calm by breaking down the mountain of work into clear, manageable checklist items."*
- **Bước 2 (Action Strategy)**: *"I double-check automated test pipelines, eliminate all non-essential meetings, and sync closely with QA engineers to catch edge-case bugs early."*
- **Bước 3 (Outcome & Reflection)**: *"This methodical approach ensures we hit our deployment deadlines without burning out the team."*
