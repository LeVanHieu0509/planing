# 🎓 BUỔI 20 - MAKING PLANS & INVITATIONS

> **Level:** L2  
> **Chủ đề:** Scheduling Meetups, Social Invitations, Accepting & Declining Politely - Lên kế hoạch hẹn gặp, gửi lời mời giao lưu, đồng ý hào hứng và từ chối lịch thiệp

---

## 📌 MỤC TIÊU BUỔI HỌC

- **Cách gửi lời mời tự nhiên & thân thiện**: Mời đi cà phê, ăn tối, tham quan triển lãm (`Would you be up for...`, `Are you free to...`, `How about we...`).
- **Đồng ý lời mời với sự hào hứng (Accepting Enthusiastically)**: `I'd love to!`, `Count me in!`, `Sounds like a plan!`, `I'm totally down!`.
- **Nghệ thuật Từ chối Lịch sự & Hẹn Dịp Khác (Declining & Rainchecks)**: `I'd love to, but unfortunately...`, `Can I take a raincheck?`, `I have a prior commitment`.
- **Thương lượng thời gian & địa điểm thuận tiện**: `What time works best for you?`, `Let's meet halfway`.
- **Thực hành phản xạ 12 câu hỏi lên lịch hẹn & giao tiếp xã hội**.

## 1. 📅 SOCIAL INVITATIONS - 12 CÂU HỎI & MẪU TRẢ LỜI 3 BƯỚC

### 🟢 Nhóm 1: Đưa ra Lời mời & Lên Kế hoạch (Making Invitations)

| # | Question | Model Answer | Grammar & Phrasing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Are you free this Saturday evening for dinner? | I'm completely free! A few friends and I were actually talking about trying out that new Italian restaurant downtown. | `be completely free` |
| 2 | Would you be up for a weekend road trip to Vung Tau? | That sounds like an amazing adventure! Who else is going, and what time should we hit the road? | Idiom: `be up for` / `hit the road` |
| 3 | How about we grab a cup of coffee tomorrow morning? | I'd love that! Does 8:30 AM at the coffee shop near your office work well for your schedule? | `Does [time] work well for you?` |
| 4 | Shall we book our movie tickets in advance? | Definitely, weekend evening screenings sell out very fast, so let's book center seats right now online. | Modal: `Shall we` |

---

### 🟡 Nhóm 2: Từ chối Lịch sự & Hẹn Dịp Khác (Declining & Rainchecks)

| # | Question | Model Answer | Grammar & Phrasing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Can you join our team karaoke night this Thursday? | Thank you so much for the invite! Unfortunately, I have a prior family commitment, but please count me in for next time! | `prior commitment` |
| 6 | Would you like to play tennis with us this Sunday? | I'd really love to, but I'm swamped with a client deadline. Can I take a raincheck for next weekend? | Idiom: `take a raincheck` |
| 7 | How do you say no without hurting a friend's feelings? | I always express gratitude first, state a genuine concise reason, and immediately propose a concrete alternative date. | `express gratitude first` |
| 8 | What should you do if an emergency forces you to cancel last minute? | Call the person immediately, apologize sincerely for the inconvenience, and offer to treat them to lunch next time. | Imperative conditional |

---

### 🔴 Nhóm 3: Thống nhất Địa điểm & Thời gian (Logistics & Coordination)

| # | Question | Model Answer | Grammar & Phrasing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | Where should we meet up to save travel time? | Since you live in District 2 and I am in District 7, let's meet halfway at a café in District 1. | `Since... let's meet halfway` |
| 10 | What is the best way to coordinate group schedules? | Setting up a quick shared messaging poll with 3 distinct time slots avoids endless back-and-forth texting. | Gerund subject: `Setting up` |
| 11 | Do you prefer rigid detailed schedules or flexible plans? | I prefer having a loose structure with fixed dinner reservations, leaving afternoon hours open for spontaneous wandering. | Contrast: `loose structure vs rigid` |
| 12 | How do you follow up after a wonderful hangout? | I always send a quick text saying: 'Thanks for such a great evening! Let's do this again soon.' | Direct quote / follow-up phrasing |

## 2. 🎯 THIS OR THAT - QUICK PICKS (PHẢN XẠ NHANH)

| Question | Model Answer | Giải thích thêm |
|---|---|---|
| **Spontaneous hangout or Planned 1 week ahead?** | I prefer **planned ahead**. | Knowing my social calendar in advance helps me manage my energy and ensures all my friends can actually attend. |
| **Big noisy party or Intimate dinner of 4?** | I prefer an **intimate dinner of 4**. | Small groups allow for meaningful, deep conversations where everyone gets to share and listen genuinely. |
| **Hosting at home or Meeting at a restaurant?** | I prefer **hosting at home**. | Cooking together with chill background music creates a warm, comfortable atmosphere with zero closing time rush. |
| **Text invitation or Phone call?** | I prefer **text invitations**. | Text messages give people time to check their calendars comfortably without the social pressure of an immediate phone reply. |

## 3. 🎭 INVITATION & RAINCHECK ROLEPLAY (MẪU TỪ CHỐI LỊCH SỰ & HẸN DỊP KHÁC)

```
Friend: "Hey Hieu! A bunch of us are going camping at Tri An Lake this Saturday. Do you want to join us?"
You:    "Oh man, that sounds like an incredible trip! I really wish I could make it, but my cousin is getting married this Saturday, so I have a prior family commitment all day."
Friend: "Ah, that's totally understandable! Family comes first."
You:    "Thanks for understanding! Can I take a raincheck? Let's definitely grab coffee next Tuesday to hear all about the camping trip!"
Friend: "You got it! Tuesday morning at 9 AM at our favorite spot. Have fun at the wedding!"
You:    "Thanks a lot! Catch you on Tuesday!"
```

## 4. 🔤 INVITATION IDIOMS & PHRASAL VERBS

```
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Những thành ngữ giao tiếp giúp lời mời và lời từ chối nghe mượt mà, văn minh.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
```

| Thành ngữ / Cụm từ | Nghĩa thực tế | Ví dụ áp dụng |
|---|---|---|
| **Take a raincheck** | Hẹn dịp khác thuận tiện hơn | _"I'm swamped today; can I **take a raincheck** for Friday?"_ |
| **Count me in** | Tôi tham gia / Cho tôi 1 suất | _"Going bowling tonight? **Count me in**!"_ |
| **Meet halfway** | Thỏa hiệp địa điểm ở giữa | _"Let's **meet halfway** at the central square."_ |
| **Tie up loose ends** | Hoàn tất các việc còn dang dở | _"I need to **tie up some loose ends** before I can leave."_ |
| **Down for it** | Rất sẵn lòng và hào hứng | _"Are you **down for** an escape room game this weekend?"_ |

## 6. 💡 PRO SPEAKING STRATEGY - THE 3-STEP RESPONSE

- **Câu hỏi**: *"How do you maintain friendships when everyone is extremely busy with their careers?"*
- **Bước 1 (Direct Philosophy)**: *"Maintaining lifelong friendships requires intentional effort rather than relying on chance."*
- **Bước 2 (Concrete Routine)**: *"Even when we cannot meet weekly, I make a habit of sending voice notes, sharing funny memes, and locking in one recurring monthly dinner date."*
- **Bước 3 (Concluding Insight)**: *"True friends understand busy seasons of life, but consistent small check-ins keep the bond evergreen."*
