> **Level:** L2  
> **Chủ đề:** Love, Relationships & Romance - Tình yêu, các giai đoạn của mối quan hệ, từ vựng cảm xúc & tình cảm

## 📌 MỤC TIÊU BUỔI HỌC

- **Nắm vững vocabulary về tình yêu & mối quan hệ**: `crush`, `in a relationship`, `break up`, `get married`, `divorce`, `cheat`, `love at first sight`.
- **Hiểu timeline của một mối quan hệ**: Từ `single` → `crush` → `dating` → `engaged` → `wedding` → `raise kids` → `grandchildren`.
- **Phân biệt từ vựng dễ nhầm**: `bride/groom`, `fiancé/fiancée`, `platonic vs romantic`, `propose vs engaged`.
- **Phát âm chuẩn cặp phụ âm**: /v/ trong *love, divorce* vs /f/ trong *fiancé, first*.
- **Trả lời câu hỏi opinion dài & tự nhiên**: Dùng công thức 3 bước Direct → Reason → Personal Example.

## 1. ❤️ LOVE CONVERSATION - 10 CÂU HỎI & MẪU TRẢ LỜI 3 BƯỚC

### 🟢 Nhóm 1: Về những người & thứ bạn yêu (People & Things You Love)

| # | Question | Model Answer | Grammar & Phrasing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tell me about the people you love. | The people I love most are my parents and my close friends. My mom is incredibly supportive — she always knows when I need encouragement. | `love most` / relative clause `who` |
| 2 | Tell me about the things you love. | I love early morning coffee, long motorbike rides, and getting completely lost in a good book on weekends. | `love` + V-ing / listing with commas |
| 3 | Do you believe in love at first sight? | Honestly, I'm a bit skeptical. I think what people call "love at first sight" is often strong **attraction**, and real love takes time to grow. | `I'm skeptical` / contrast: `but real love...` |
| 4 | What is more important — love or money? Why? | I think love lays the foundation, but financial stability is what keeps the structure standing. Without money, even the deepest love faces enormous daily stress. | Metaphor: `foundation` / `structure` |

> **💡 Mẹo nói tự nhiên:** Khi nói về người mình yêu, hãy dùng tính từ cụ thể thay vì "nice" hay "good":
> - ❌ "I love my mom. She is very nice."
> - ✅ "I love my mom. She is incredibly **supportive** and always gives me the most **grounded** advice."

## 2. 📊 RELATIONSHIP TIMELINE - DÒNG THỜI GIAN TÌNH YÊU

```
SINGLE → CRUSH → ASK OUT → 1ST DATE → BF/GF → PROPOSE → ENGAGED
       ↓                                         ↓
 "Would you like                             BREAK UP
  to go on a date?"                          BROKE UP
                                                 ↓
FIANCE (fee-on-SAY) → WEDDING → HONEYMOON → HAVE BABY → RAISE KIDS
                         ↓
                   GIRL = BRIDE
                   MAN  = GROOM         → GRANDCHILDREN → YOU DIE! 😅

⚠️ Nhánh tiêu cực: DIVORCE / CHEAT / (platonic - not romantic)
```

> **💡 Lưu ý phát âm quan trọng:**
> - **Fiancé** (nam) / **Fiancée** (nữ) → đọc là /fiˈɒn.seɪ/ = **"fee-on-SAY"** (nhấn âm cuối)
> - **Propose** ≠ **Engaged**: Propose là **hành động** cầu hôn; Engaged là **trạng thái** đã đính hôn.

## 7. 💡 PRO SPEAKING STRATEGY - CÔNG THỨC MỞ RỘNG CÂU 3 BƯỚC

> **Công thức 3 Bước:** `Direct Answer` ➔ `Reason / Explanation` ➔ `Personal Example`

- **Câu hỏi**: *"What are the pros and cons of being single?"*
- **Bước 1 (Direct)**: *"Being single has real advantages, but it also comes with genuine downsides."*
- **Bước 2 (Reason)**: *"On the positive side, you have total freedom to focus on your goals, travel spontaneously, and invest in yourself. But on the flip side, loneliness can really creep in, especially during life milestones when you want someone to share the moment with."*
- **Bước 3 (Example)**: *"Personally, I went through a two-year period of being single after university, and while I grew enormously as a person, there were definitely quiet nights when I missed having that special someone."*
