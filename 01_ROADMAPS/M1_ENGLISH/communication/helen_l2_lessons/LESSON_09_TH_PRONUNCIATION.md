# 🎓 BUỔI 9 - /TH/ PRONUNCIATION & TH QUESTIONS

> **Level:** L2  
> **Chủ đề:** Phát âm chuẩn âm /TH/ (Voiceless /θ/ vs Voiced /ð/) & Luyện tập câu hỏi

---

## 📌 MỤC TIÊU BUỔI HỌC

- Sửa lỗi phát âm âm **/TH/** phổ biến của người Việt (hay phát âm nhầm thành /t/, /d/, /s/ hoặc /z/).
- Phân biệt 2 loại âm **/TH/**: **Voiceless /θ/** (không rung) và **Voiced /ð/** (có rung).
- Luyện tập 8 câu mẫu chuẩn và trả lời 10 câu hỏi giao tiếp chứa nhiều âm /TH/.

---

## 1. 👅 QUY TẮC VÀNG PHÁT ÂM ÂM /TH/

> ⚠️ **QUY TẮC BẮT BUỘC**: _"THE ONLY WAY TO MAKE THE /TH/ SOUND IS TO STICK YOUR TONGUE OUT! DON'T BE SHY!"_  
> 👉 **Phải thè đầu lưỡi ra giữa 2 hàm răng khi phát âm!**

---

### 🅰️ Voiceless /θ/ - Âm không rung (Blow, don't vibrate)

- **Cách làm**: Đặt đầu lưỡi giữa 2 răng, thổi luồng hơi nhẹ ra ngoài, dây thanh quản **không rung**.

| Words (Từ mẫu) | Meaning         | Transcription |
| -------------- | --------------- | ------------- |
| **Thief**      | Kẻ trộm         | /θiːf/        |
| **Thick**      | Dày             | /θɪk/         |
| **Thousand**   | Hàng ngàn       | /ˈθaʊ.zənd/   |
| **Thursday**   | Thứ năm         | /ˈθɜːz.deɪ/   |
| **Three**      | Số 3            | /θriː/        |
| **Thank**      | Cảm ơn          | /θæŋk/        |
| **Teeth**      | Răng (số nhiều) | /tiːθ/        |
| **Thirsty**    | Khát nước       | /ˈθɜː.sti/    |

---

### 🅱️ Voiced /ð/ - Âm có rung (Vibrate, don't blow)

- **Cách làm**: Đặt đầu lưỡi giữa 2 răng, phát âm rừ rừ làm **rung dây thanh quản**.

| Words (Từ mẫu) | Meaning       | Transcription |
| -------------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| **Clothes**    | Quần áo       | /kləʊðz/      |
| **Father**     | Bố            | /ˈfɑː.ðər/    |
| **Weather**    | Thời tiết     | /ˈweð.ər/     |
| **Them**       | Họ / Chúng    | /ðem/         |
| **Feather**    | Lông vũ       | /ˈfeð.ər/     |
| **That**       | Cái đó        | /ðæt/         |
| **Those**      | Những cái đó  | /ðəʊz/        |
| **Breathe**    | Thở (động từ) | /briːð/       |

## 2. 🗣️ SENTENCE PRACTICE - LUYỆN ĐỌC 8 CÂU MẪU

1. **Thank** (/θ/) you so much for **thinking** (/θ/) of me.
2. I spent more **than** (/ð/) $1,000 on **clothes** (/ð/) **this** (/ð/) **month** (/θ/).
3. **That** (/ð/) man is my **mother's** (/ð/) **brother** (/ð/).
4. My **father** (/ð/) **threw** (/θ/) the ball at me.
5. I have **thirty-two** (/θ/) **teeth** (/θ/).
6. **The** (/ð/) cat **thinks** (/θ/) it's a dog.
7. **This** (/ð/) school is **the** (/ð/) best **thing** (/θ/) in Saigon.
8. **Thirty-three** (/θ/) **thousand** (/θ/) people **think** (/θ/) **that** (/ð/) **Thursday** (/θ/) is **their** (/ð/) **thirtieth** (/θ/) birthday.

## 3. ❓ TH QUESTIONS & ANSWERS - CÂU HỎI & MẪU TRẢ LỜI

### 📊 Bảng câu hỏi & Mẫu trả lời tham khảo:

| #   | Question                                                                  | Model Answer                                                                            | Focus Sounds                                 |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Which part of Vietnam has the best weather?**                           | In my opinion, Da Lat or Central Highlands has the best **weather** because it is cool. | `weather` (/ð/)                              |
| 2   | **What did you do for your last birthday?**                               | For my last **birthday**, I went out for dinner with my close friends.                  | `birthday` (/θ/)                             |
| 3   | **What's your favorite thing to do on the weekends?**                     | My favorite **thing** is learning new tech frameworks and practicing English speaking.  | `thing` (/θ/)                                |
| 4   | **Tell me some healthy things you do every day.**                         | I drink **three** liters of water and try to sleep before 11 PM every night.            | `things` (/θ/), `three` (/θ/)                |
| 5   | **What are some difficult things about your job?**                        | The most difficult **thing** about software engineering is debugging complex issues.    | `thing` (/θ/)                                |
| 6   | **What do you think about Saigon? How does it compare to your hometown?** | I **think** Saigon is very active, while my hometown is quiet and peaceful.             | `think` (/θ/)                                |
| 7   | **How is clothing and fashion in Saigon different from the countryside?** | Fashion in Saigon is more modern and diverse **than** in the countryside.               | `than` (/ð/)                                 |
| 8   | **Is the movie theater a good place for a first date?**                   | I **think** a quiet café is better **than** a movie **theater** for a first date.       | `think` (/θ/), `than` (/ð/), `theater` (/θ/) |
| 9   | **Who do you spend your free time with?**                                 | I usually spend my free time **with** my close friends or colleagues.                   | `with` (/ð/)                                 |
| 10  | **What would you do with a thousand dollars?**                            | If I had a **thousand** dollars, I would upgrade my laptop and buy courses!             | `thousand` (/θ/)                             |
