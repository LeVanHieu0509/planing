## 2. 🔺 GIỚI TỪ THỜI GIAN - IN / ON / AT / TO

**Mẹo: Nhớ theo hình kim tự tháp - từ RỘNG → HẸP**

```
        ╱ IN ╲           → Rộng nhất (Year, Month, Time of Day)
       ╱  ON  ╲          → Vừa (Day, Date)
      ╱   AT   ╲         → Hẹp nhất (Time cụ thể)
     ╱    TO    ╲        → Di chuyển (Movement)
```

| Giới từ | Dùng cho                   | Ví dụ                                         | Mẹo nhớ                               |
| ------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| **IN**  | Year / Month / Time of Day | "I usually take a shower **in** the morning." | **IN** = **I**n khoảng thời gian rộng |
| **ON**  | Day / Date                 | "I don't work **on** the weekends."           | **ON** = **O**n a specific day        |
| **AT**  | Time (giờ cụ thể)          | "I wake up **at** 7:00 AM."                   | **AT** = **A**t a precise **T**ime    |
| **TO**  | Movement verbs (di chuyển) | "I go **to** the coffeeshop every day."       | **TO** = đi **TO**ới đâu đó           |

### Chi tiết từng giới từ:

#### 📦 IN - Khoảng thời gian rộng

```
IN the morning / IN the afternoon / IN the evening
IN January / IN July / IN 2026
IN summer / IN winter
```

#### 📅 ON - Ngày cụ thể

```
ON Monday / ON Friday
ON the weekend(s)
ON July 21st / ON my birthday
ON New Year's Day
```

#### ⏰ AT - Giờ cụ thể

```
AT 7:00 AM / AT 9:30 PM
AT noon / AT midnight
AT night (⚠️ ngoại lệ: AT night, không phải IN night)
```

#### 🚶 TO - Di chuyển

```
Go TO work / Go TO school
Go TO the coffeeshop
Go TO the gym
```

### ⚠️ Ngoại lệ hay nhầm:

| Hay nhầm          | Đúng                  | Mẹo                            |
| ----------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| ❌ IN night       | ✅ **AT** night       | AT night = ngoại lệ, phải nhớ  |
| ❌ ON the morning | ✅ **IN** the morning | Morning/Afternoon/Evening = IN |
| ❌ IN 7:00 AM     | ✅ **AT** 7:00 AM     | Giờ cụ thể = AT                |
| ❌ IN Monday      | ✅ **ON** Monday      | Thứ trong tuần = ON            |

## 3. 🧠 MẸO GHI NHỚ TỔNG HỢP

### Mẹo 1: Nhớ giới từ bằng size (to → nhỏ)

```
IN  = hộp TO   (year, month)    → thời gian dài, rộng
ON  = trên BỀ MẶT (day, date)  → 1 ngày cụ thể
AT  = 1 ĐIỂM (time)            → 1 thời điểm chính xác
TO  = MŨI TÊN → (movement)    → di chuyển tới đâu
```

### Mẹo 2: Nhớ daily activities bằng timeline

```
6:00  → Wake up, Get up, Stretch
6:15  → Take a shower, Brush teeth
6:45  → Eat breakfast, Watch TV
8:00  → Go to work, Ride a motorbike
12:00 → Eat lunch
18:00 → Go to the coffeeshop
19:00 → Eat dinner
21:00 → Read a book
22:30 → Go to bed, Sleep 💤
```

### Mẹo 3: Phân biệt hay nhầm

| Hay nhầm                             | Cách nhớ                                                              |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Wake up** vs **Get up**            | Wake up = mở mắt / Get up = ra khỏi giường                            |
| **Take a shower** vs **Take a bath** | Shower = vòi sen (đứng) / Bath = bồn tắm (ngồi)                       |
| **Go to bed** vs **Sleep**           | Go to bed = lên giường / Sleep = ngủ (có thể chưa ngủ khi lên giường) |
| **AT night** vs **IN the evening**   | Evening = buổi tối sớm (6-9pm) / Night = đêm khuya                    |
| **"Took a shower"**                  | Quá khứ của Take → **Took** (bất quy tắc)                             |

## 4. 🏋️ BÀI TẬP LUYỆN NÓI - 12 Questions

### Questions từ worksheet:

| #   | Question                                      | Gợi ý trả lời                                                                           |
| --- | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | What do you do every day?                     | "I wake up **at** 6 AM. I take a shower **in** the morning. I go **to** work **at** 8." |
| 2   | What do you usually do on your days off?      | "**On** my days off, I usually sleep **in** and go **to** the coffeeshop."              |
| 3   | What time do you usually wake up?             | "I usually wake up **at** 6:30 AM."                                                     |
| 4   | Where do you spend most of your time?         | "I spend most of my time **at** home / **at** the office."                              |
| 5   | What is your favorite time of the day?        | "My favorite time is **in** the evening, because I can relax."                          |
| 6   | What do you usually do before bed?            | "I usually read a book or watch YouTube before bed."                                    |
| 7   | What do you usually do at night?              | "**At** night, I study English and code."                                               |
| 8   | What do you usually do on the weekend?        | "**On** the weekend, I play football and go **to** the gym."                            |
| 9   | What do you usually do at noon?               | "**At** noon, I eat lunch and take a short nap."                                        |
| 10  | Describe your morning routine.                | "I wake up **at** 6. I stretch, take a shower, brush my teeth, and eat breakfast."      |
| 11  | What is your favorite daily activity and why? | "My favorite activity is coding because I love solving problems."                       |
| 12  | Tell me about your perfect day.               | "**In** the morning, I wake up **at** 7. I go **to** the gym, eat a big breakfast..."   |

## 5. 📝 MẪU TRẢ LỜI HOÀN CHỈNH - Describe your perfect day

```
"My perfect day starts at 7 AM. In the morning, I wake up,
stretch, and take a shower. I eat breakfast at home.

At 9, I go to the coffeeshop and work on my laptop.
I eat lunch at noon with my friends.

In the afternoon, I go to the gym and exercise for one hour.

In the evening, I eat dinner at a nice restaurant.
At night, I read a book and go to bed at 10:30 PM."
```

> **Mẹo:** Khi nói, dùng **time markers** (In the morning, At noon, In the evening, At night) để câu chuyện mạch lạc hơn.
