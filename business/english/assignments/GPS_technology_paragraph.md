**Topic:** Arguments for and against GPS technology

## 📝 NOTES FROM EXERCISE 3

**Main idea:** GPS is a good idea for anyone who travels a lot

**Arguments FOR (pros):**

- GPS maps are always up-to-date and correct
- Safer to use when driving
- Saves time (and money on petrol)

**Arguments AGAINST (cons):**

- More expensive than a normal map

---

## ✍️ PARAGRAPH

GPS is a good idea for anyone who travels a lot. **Firstly**, GPS maps are always up-to-date and correct, so drivers always have the most accurate information about roads and routes. **In addition**, GPS is much safer to use when driving because you do not need to look at a paper map while you are on the road. **For example**, with voice navigation, you can keep your eyes on the road at all times. **As a result**, GPS saves both time and money on petrol, since it always calculates the fastest and most efficient route. **However**, GPS devices can be more expensive than a normal paper map. **On the other hand**, if you travel frequently, the time and fuel savings more than make up for the extra cost. **In other words**, for regular travellers, GPS is an excellent and worthwhile investment in modern technology.
