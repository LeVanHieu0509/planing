# SCRIPT: IDEAL JOB & WORKING ENVIRONMENT (Video Assignment)

**Topic:** Record a video in which you present your ideal job and working environment.
**Theme:** Coffee Shop Startup & Working Environment for Students
**Speaker:** Hieu Le Van 

---

## 💡 TIPS TRƯỚC KHI QUAY
1. **Phong thái:** Tự tin, mỉm cười tự nhiên, thể hiện sự hiếu khách và năng lượng trẻ trung của một founder quán cà phê.
2. **Eye contact:** Nhìn thẳng vào ống kính camera, thỉnh thoảng liếc kịch bản.
3. **Phát âm:** Nhấn mạnh vào các key words (từ in đậm) để bài nói có ngữ điệu (intonation).

---

## 🎬 KỊCH BẢN (SCRIPT)

**[Introduction]**
Hello everyone! My name is Hieu. Today, I am very excited to share with you my **ideal job** and **working environment**. 

While many people dream of working in big corporate offices, my dream job is to become an **entrepreneur** and open my own **coffee shop startup**. 

**[The Ideal Job]**
In my ideal job, I want to be a business owner who creates value for the community. I love the idea of building a unique coffee brand from scratch—from designing the menu and selecting the best coffee beans, to decorating the space. Running a coffee shop allows me to blend my passion for business with my love for creating a welcoming atmosphere for people to connect, study, and relax. 

**[The Ideal Working Environment]**
When it comes to the working environment, my vision is to build a **dynamic and supportive** workplace, especially for university students. 

First, I want to offer a highly **flexible schedule**. I understand that students have to balance their studies and part-time jobs, so the working hours must be **adaptable** to their university timetables. I want them to earn an income without sacrificing their education.

Second, the environment must be **youthful, energetic, and collaborative**. I want my coffee shop to be a place where young people can learn essential soft skills like customer service, teamwork, and problem-solving. Instead of a strict boss-employee relationship, I want to be a **mentor** who **empowers** my staff and treats them like a family. 

**[Conclusion]**
In short, my ideal career is running a successful coffee shop where I can not only serve great coffee but also create an inspiring and flexible working environment for young students. 

Thank you for listening!

## 📚 TỪ VỰNG ĂN ĐIỂM (Vocabulary to highlight)
*   **Entrepreneur:** Doanh nhân, người khởi nghiệp.
*   **Dynamic and supportive:** Năng động và mang tính hỗ trợ.
*   **Flexible schedule:** Lịch làm việc linh hoạt.
*   **Adaptable to timetables:** Có thể thích ứng/điều chỉnh theo thời gian biểu (trường học).
*   **Youthful, energetic, and collaborative:** Trẻ trung, tràn đầy năng lượng và có tính hợp tác.
*   **Mentor:** Người cố vấn, người hướng dẫn.
*   **Empower:** Trao quyền, tạo động lực.
*   **From scratch:** Bắt đầu từ con số không / từ bàn tay trắng.
